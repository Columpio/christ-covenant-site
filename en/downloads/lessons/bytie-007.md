## 4) The importance of understanding the Trinity of the Holy, True God

### (1) The trinity of God means that God is one in numerical terms, and that there are three eternal differences in the Essence of the One God – the Hypostasis (Personality) of God the Father, the Hypostasis (Personality)God of the Word, the Hypostasis (Personality) of God the Holy Spirit, each of which is God and equal in Power and Glory, manifesting Themselves simultaneously and jointly in three Hypostases (Personalities), having no possibility of separation within Himself, He is the Holy and Indivisible Trinity (the Omnipotent, Omniscient, Omnipresent Absolute of God). 1 John 5:7,Heb. 1:3
> For there are three that bear record in heaven, the Father, the Word, and the Holy Ghost: and these three are one. (1 John 5:7, KJV)
> Who being the brightness of his glory, and the express image of his person, and upholding all things by the word of his power, when he had by himself purged our sins, sat down on the right hand of the Majesty on high; (Heb. 1:3, KJV)

#### (a) Personality is an individual object that exists by itself

#### (b) A hypostasis is a personal manifestation of the One God in the being, with all personal characteristics (thinking, will, emotions, that is, the same as a Personality), except for the possibility of AUTONOMOUS EXISTENCE, Hypostases are different, but inseparable – these are not three gods, but ONE GOD who expresses Himself in three Hypostases

## 5) The importance of understanding the functional priority of Hypostasis in the Holy, Triune God (human example)

### (1) The trinity of man means that there are three eternal differences in the essence of man – body, soul and spirit (the image and likeness of God)

#### (a) Physical work is the work of the body (the participation of the soul and spirit)

#### (b) Emotions are the work of the soul (involving the body and spirit)

#### (c) Transmission of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ – the work of the spirit (participation of body and soul)

#### (d) The functional priority of action in a triune person is either spirit, soul, or body, functional, but not essential priority in a triune person – everything is important

### (2) The trinity of God means that there are three eternal differences in the essence of God – the hypostasis of God the Father, the Hypostasis of God the Word, the Hypostasis of God the Holy Spirit

#### (a) The hypostasis of God the Father (as the soul in man) – gives birth to the Word, the Son (He is the Father), confirms God's will (participation of the Hypostasis of God the Word and the Hypostasis of God the Holy Spirit) John 6:38,Acts 2:23 (Rev. 13:8,1 Pet. 1:18-20)
> For I came down from heaven, not to do mine own will, but the will of him that sent me. (John 6:38, KJV)
> Him, being delivered by the determinate counsel and foreknowledge of God, ye have taken, and by wicked hands have crucified and slain: (Acts 2:23, KJV)
> And all that dwell upon the earth shall worship him, whose names are not written in the book of life of the Lamb slain from the foundation of the world. (Rev. 13:8, KJV)
> Forasmuch as ye know that ye were not redeemed with corruptible things, as silver and gold, from your vain conversation received by tradition from your fathers; But with the precious blood of Christ, as of a lamb without blemish and without spot: Who verily was foreordained before the foundation of the world, but was manifest in these last times for you, (1 Pet. 1:18-20, KJV)

#### (b) The hypostasis of God the Word, the Son (as the body in man) – is born of the Father (He is the Son), shows God's will (the participation of the Hypostasis of God the Father and the Hypostasis of God the Holy Spirit) John 1:18; 6:38
> No man hath seen God at any time; the only begotten Son, which is in the bosom of the Father, he hath declared him. (John 1:18, KJV)
> For I came down from heaven, not to do mine own will, but the will of him that sent me. (John 6:38, KJV)

#### (c) The hypostasis of God the Holy Spirit (as the spirit in man) – comes from the Father and the Son, explains and implements God's will (the participation of the Hypostasis of God the Father and the Hypostasis of God the Word) John 15:26,Acts 1:8,Heb. 9:14
> But when the Comforter is come, whom I will send unto you from the Father, even the Spirit of truth, which proceedeth from the Father, he shall testify of me: (John 15:26, KJV)
> But ye shall receive power, after that the Holy Ghost is come upon you: and ye shall be witnesses unto me both in Jerusalem, and in all Judæa, and in Samaria, and unto the uttermost part of the earth. (Acts 1:8, KJV)
> How much more shall the blood of Christ, who through the eternal Spirit offered himself without spot to God, purge your conscience from dead works to serve the living God? (Heb. 9:14, KJV)

#### (d) The functional priority of action in the Triune God is either the Hypostasis of God the Father, or the Hypostasis of God the Word, or the Hypostasis of God the Holy Spirit, i.e., the functional, rather than essential priority in the Holy, Triune God is All equal, a manifestation of the Holy, The Triune God simultaneously and jointly in three Hypostases without the possibility of separation in Himself – God the Father, God the Word, God the Holy Spirit, the Holy and Indivisible Trinity

## 6) Misunderstanding of the Trinity of God

### (1) The heresy of polytheism (Tritheism) – the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit are three different, separate and independent gods. (Belief in the heresy of polytheism is faith not in the God of the Bible, i.e. faith in another god, sin to death)

### (2) The heresy of Oneness (Sabellianism) is the denial of three eternal differences in the being of the Holy, Triune God

#### (a) The Father is the Son, the Word and the Holy Spirit, the Son, the Word is the Father and the Holy Spirit, the Holy Spirit is the Father and the Son – the words are synonymous. (A body is not a soul or a spirit, a soul is not a body or a spirit, a spirit is not a body or a soul – three eternal differences in the human being, the denial of three eternal differences in the human being is not a person, but a ghost)

#### (b) Understanding the appearance and action of God alternately in different persons – the action of the Father, there is no Son and the Holy Spirit, the action of the Son, there is no Father and the Holy Spirit, the action of the Holy Spirit, there is no Father and the Son (example of three different masks)

#### (c) The denial of Personal characteristics in the Hypostasis of God the Holy Spirit – "divine power and energy." (Faith in the oneness heresy is faith not in the God of the Bible, i.e. faith in another god, sin to death)
