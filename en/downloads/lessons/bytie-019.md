## 3) The importance of accepting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ

### (1) The atonement of all the sins of all people is the legitimate basis for the forgiveness of every person (execution of the sentence of the Word of God)

### (2) Redemption without forgiveness is not effective for man. (Atonement is the legitimate basis for human forgiveness)

### (3) Forgiveness is the acceptance of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, the confession of man

#### (a) Who is Christ? – acceptance of the Divinity of Christ

#### (b) The purpose of His visit? – acceptance of the All-sufficiency of Christ's Sacrifice (execution of the sentence of the Word of God by Christ instead of man)

### (4) Unforgiven or unforgiven sin – the impossibility of legitimate justification of man, eternal separation from God
- The importance of Understanding Salvation in V.Z. and N.Z.
- V.Z. – faith in the coming Christ (God in the flesh) and the All-sufficiency of His Sacrifice (Covenant, Christ, animal sacrifice)
- N.Z. – faith in the coming Jesus as Christ (God in the flesh) and the All-sufficiency of His Sacrifice (the Covenant, Christ, the Breaking of Bread)
