## 8) Practical issues for personal evangelization and testing of every person

### (1) Do you believe in God? – the importance of understanding a person’s general position in relation to God

### (2) Which God do you believe in? – the importance of understanding a person's faith

### (3) If you die now (heart attack), will you be with God? – verification of the understanding of salvation, the hope of man (against the heresy of the "mixed gospel")

### (4) Can there be curses and demons in those born again? – verification of the understanding of salvation, the hope of man (against the heresy of "partial salvation")

### (5) What is the purpose of the people of God (the Church of Christ)? – verification of the understanding of the destiny of the people of God (against a misunderstanding of the destiny of the Church of Christ)
