## 2) The importance of proper understanding of the Sacrament

### (1) Complete Transubstantiation (Western theology) – the acceptance of bread and wine as the actual body and blood of Christ

### (2) Partial transubstantiation (Eastern theology) – the acceptance of bread and wine as the actual body and blood of Christ, with the visible and tangible qualities of bread and wine

### (3) Denial of transubstantiation (Protest. theology) – the acceptance of bread and wine as a PROTOTYPE of the body and blood of Christ

#### (a) God's prohibition is man's flesh and blood. Gen. 9:3-6
> Every moving thing that liveth shall be meat for you; even as the green herb have I given you all things. But flesh with the life thereof, which is the blood thereof, shall ye not eat. And surely your blood of your lives will I require; at the hand of every beast will I require it, and at the hand of man; at the hand of every man’s brother will I require the life of man. Whoso sheddeth man’s blood, by man shall his blood be shed: for in the image of God made he man. (Gen. 9:3-6, KJV)

## 4) The unworthy performance of Communion

### (1) Misunderstanding of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ (the essence of sin)

#### (a) A rite is a meaningless act without understanding the Covenant, Christ

#### (b) Mysticism – the desire for a person to receive visible answers in a supernatural way, without understanding the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ

### (2) The denial of the Divinity of Christ is the heretical teaching of "Arianism", the rejection of the Divinity of Christ

#### (a) Denying the Divinity of Christ – there is no right to be responsible for all people and all of God's creation

#### (b) Denial of the Divinity of Christ – there is no redemption and salvation of mankind and all of God's creation

### (3) The denial of the fact of the forgiveness of ALL human sins through the acceptance of Christ's Sacrifice is the heretical teaching of the "mixed gospel", a conscious rejection of the All–sufficiency of Christ's Sacrifice. (An unforgiven or unforgiven sin is the impossibility of a person's legitimate justification, eternal separation from God)

#### (a) Communion is the confession of sins and God's method of solution. (An attempt at purification outside of the Sacrifice and Asceticism of Christ is heresy)

#### (b) Communion is the affirmation of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, and a constant reminder of the reason for the righteousness and holiness of God's people.

#### (c) The prohibition of participation in the Communion of children of God is a matter of Law, religion. (There has never been, there is not, and there will not be worthy ones). 1 Cor. 11:23-32
