## 5) The history of the Church, the fate of the disciples

### (1) Peter preached in Jerusalem, Judea, Antioch, Bethany, Asia, Italy, Rome. He was crucified upside down in 67 A.D. by order of Nero

### (2) John the Theologian preached in Jerusalem, Ephesus, and many Asian cities. During the persecution of Christians, he was brought to Rome and sentenced to death. After a bowl of poison and an attempt to boil John in boiling oil, he was exiled to the island of Patmos.
- After a long exile, John was freed and returned to Ephesus, where he died a natural death around 101 A.D.

### (3) Andrey – preaching in Greece, Turkey, Crimea, Kiev. He was crucified on an X-shaped cross in the Greek city of Patras in 67 A.D.

### (4) James Zavadeev – Sermon in Jerusalem (Epistle of James) beheaded in Jerusalem by order of Herod Agrippa in 44 A.D.

### (5) Philip – preaching in the Assyrian countries. He was crucified upside down in the city of Hierapolis (Turkey) in 90 A.D.

### (6) Bartholomew – preaching in Asia, India, Armenia
- Martyrdom in the city of Alban (Armenia)

### (7) Thomas the Sermon in many Asian countries and in India
- Martyrdom in India in 68 A.D.

### (8) Matthew (the Evangelist) – preaching in Judea and Ethiopia
- He was killed by a sword in one of the cities of Ethiopia in 74 A.D.

### (9) James Alphaeus – preaching in Syria and Egypt. He was crucified in Egypt in 62 A.D., martyred.

### (10) Judas Thaddeus (Yakovlev) – preaching in Judea, Galilee, Samaria, Idumea, Syria, Arabia, Messopotamia. He was hanged on a cross and shot with arrows in Armenia in 72 A.D.

### (11) Simon the Canaanite (Zealot) – preaching in Mauritania and England. Martyrdom by crucifixion or sawn through with a saw on the Black Sea coast of the Caucasus in 107 A.D.

### (12) Matthew – preaching in Judea and Ethiopia. Stoned and beheaded in Judea in 63 A.D.

### (13) Luke preached in Achaia, Egypt, and Libya. He was hanged from a tree in the Greek city of Thebes in 84 A.D.

### (14) Mark (evangelist) – preaching in Alexandria (Egypt), Libya, Africa. Died during the persecution of a crowd of pagans in Alexandria in 68 AD
