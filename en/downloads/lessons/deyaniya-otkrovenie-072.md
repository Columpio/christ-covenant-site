> After these things Paul departed from Athens, and came to Corinth; And found a certain Jew named Aquila, born in Pontus, lately come from Italy, with his wife Priscilla; (because that Claudius had commanded all Jews to depart from Rome:) and came unto them. And because he was of the same craft, he abode with them, and wrought: for by their occupation they were tentmakers. And he reasoned in the synagogue every sabbath, and persuaded the Jews and the Greeks. And when Silas and Timotheus were come from Macedonia, Paul was pressed in the spirit, and testified to the Jews that Jesus was Christ. And when they opposed themselves, and blasphemed, he shook his raiment, and said unto them, Your blood be upon your own heads; I am clean: from henceforth I will go unto the Gentiles. And he departed thence, and entered into a certain man’s house, named Justus, one that worshipped God, whose house joined hard to the synagogue. And Crispus, the chief ruler of the synagogue, believed on the Lord with all his house; and many of the Corinthians hearing believed, and were baptized. Then spake the Lord to Paul in the night by a vision, Be not afraid, but speak, and hold not thy peace: For I am with thee, and no man shall set on thee to hurt thee: for I have much people in this city. And he continued there a year and six months, teaching the word of God among them. And when Gallio was the deputy of Achaia, the Jews made insurrection with one accord against Paul, and brought him to the judgment seat, Saying, This fellow persuadeth men to worship God contrary to the law. And when Paul was now about to open his mouth, Gallio said unto the Jews, If it were a matter of wrong or wicked lewdness, O ye Jews, reason would that I should bear with you: But if it be a question of words and names, and of your law, look ye to it; for I will be no judge of such matters. And he drave them from the judgment seat. Then all the Greeks took Sosthenes, the chief ruler of the synagogue, and beat him before the judgment seat. And Gallio cared for none of those things. And Paul after this tarried there yet a good while, and then took his leave of the brethren, and sailed thence into Syria, and with him Priscilla and Aquila; having shorn his head in Cenchrea: for he had a vow. ## 8) 1 Cor. 11, - inaccurate understanding of the Participle

### (1) The importance of understanding Communion is to affirm participation in the Covenant with God through the Sacrifice and Asceticism of Jesus Christ

### (2) The importance of reasoning about the Body and Blood of Jesus Christ, His Sacrifice – confidence in the forgiveness of all sins and salvation, the affirmation of the all-sufficiency of the Sacrifice of Jesus Christ and the solution of all human problems (the power of sin, death, curses)

### (3) Misunderstanding and misunderstanding of Communion – there is no salvation and rebirth, the importance of accepting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ

## 9) 1 Cor. 12-14, - inaccurate understanding of the gifts of the Holy Spirit

### (1) The importance of understanding the meaning of the gifts of the Holy Spirit – the knowledge and transmission of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, the disciples, Worldwide Evangelization, service to people, the importance of edification and spiritual growth of man

### (2) The importance of understanding God's sovereignty – the presence and distribution of the gifts of the Holy Spirit, God's decision and will

### (3) The importance of understanding the meaninglessness of the gifts of the Holy Spirit without the action of God's love – service to God and man

### (4) The importance of understanding the conformity of the actions of the gifts of the Holy Spirit to the ordinances of the Word of God

## 10) 1 Cor. 15, - inaccurate understanding of the resurrection

### (1) The meaninglessness of Christianity, the sacrifice of Christ without resurrection from the dead – "the victory of sin, death and hell, Satan"

### (2) The statement of the absence of resurrection – the humiliation of Christ and His Sacrifice, the misunderstanding of Creation, the Fall, Restoration, the importance of accepting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ

### (3) The importance of understanding the future, the resurrection from the dead, and the life of the next age is God's will for every human being.
