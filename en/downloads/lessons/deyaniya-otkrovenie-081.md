### (2) Gal. 5:2-6, - the importance of accepting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ
> Behold, I Paul say unto you, that if ye be circumcised, Christ shall profit you nothing. For I testify again to every man that is circumcised, that he is a debtor to do the whole law. Christ is become of no effect unto you, whosoever of you are justified by the law; ye are fallen from grace. For we through the Spirit wait for the hope of righteousness by faith. For in Jesus Christ neither circumcision availeth any thing, nor uncircumcision; but faith which worketh by love. (Gal. 5:2-6, KJV)

#### (a) Gal. 5:2-3, - an attempt at salvation through the "mixed gospel" obliges a person to fulfill the entire law and testifies to the rejection of the Sacrifice and the Feat of Christ
> Behold, I Paul say unto you, that if ye be circumcised, Christ shall profit you nothing. For I testify again to every man that is circumcised, that he is a debtor to do the whole law. (Gal. 5:2-3, KJV)

#### (b) Gal. 5:4, - the attempt at salvation through the "mixed gospel" (false teaching) testifies to the rejection of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ and falling away from salvation
> Christ is become of no effect unto you, whosoever of you are justified by the law; ye are fallen from grace. (Gal. 5:4, KJV)

#### (c) Gal. 5:4, - the importance of understanding the essence of the problem of the Galatian Churches, the constant conflict of Religion and the Good News
> Christ is become of no effect unto you, whosoever of you are justified by the law; ye are fallen from grace. (Gal. 5:4, KJV)

#### (d) Gal. 5:5-6, - the importance of preserving the purity of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ (the faith of God), the importance of fulfilling the destiny of the children of God (Knowledge and transmission of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, Worldwide Evangelization, the coming of Christ)

### (5) Gal. 5:16-23, - the importance of knowing and transmitting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ
> This I say then, Walk in the Spirit, and ye shall not fulfil the lust of the flesh. For the flesh lusteth against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh: and these are contrary the one to the other: so that ye cannot do the things that ye would. But if ye be led of the Spirit, ye are not under the law. Now the works of the flesh are manifest, which are these; Adultery, fornication, uncleanness, lasciviousness, Idolatry, witchcraft, hatred, variance, emulations, wrath, strife, seditions, heresies, Envyings, murders, drunkenness, revellings, and such like: of the which I tell you before, as I have also told you in time past, that they which do such things shall not inherit the kingdom of God. But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith, Meekness, temperance: against such there is no law. (Gal. 5:16-23, KJV)

#### (a) Gal. 5:16, - life according to the spirit, fulfillment of destiny, knowledge and transmission of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, the disciples, Worldwide Evangelization
> This I say then, Walk in the Spirit, and ye shall not fulfil the lust of the flesh. (Gal. 5:16, KJV)

#### (b) Gal. 5:16, - the importance of fulfilling the destiny, God's condition for the sanctification and spiritual growth of God's people
> This I say then, Walk in the Spirit, and ye shall not fulfil the lust of the flesh. (Gal. 5:16, KJV)

#### (c) Gal. 5:17, - understanding the conflict between the spirit and the flesh, understanding the influence of the remnants of the sinful essence on a person. (Rebirth of soul and spirit, change of body in the future, resurrection from the dead, completion of salvation). Rom. 7:19-25
> For the flesh lusteth against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh: and these are contrary the one to the other: so that ye cannot do the things that ye would. (Gal. 5:17, KJV)
> For the good that I would I do not: but the evil which I would not, that I do. Now if I do that I would not, it is no more I that do it, but sin that dwelleth in me. I find then a law, that, when I would do good, evil is present with me. For I delight in the law of God after the inward man: But I see another law in my members, warring against the law of my mind, and bringing me into captivity to the law of sin which is in my members. O wretched man that I am! who shall deliver me from the body of this death? I thank God through Jesus Christ our Lord. So then with the mind I myself serve the law of God; but with the flesh the law of sin. (Rom. 7:19-25, KJV)
