# Eph. 1
## 1.1) Eph. 1:1-14, - the importance of understanding the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ
> Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ by the will of God, to the saints which are at Ephesus, and to the faithful in Christ Jesus: Grace be to you, and peace, from God our Father, and from the Lord Jesus Christ. Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who hath blessed us with all spiritual blessings in heavenly places in Christ: According as he hath chosen us in him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and without blame before him in love: Having predestinated us unto the adoption of children by Jesus Christ to himself, according to the good pleasure of his will, To the praise of the glory of his grace, wherein he hath made us accepted in the beloved. In whom we have redemption through his blood, the forgiveness of sins, according to the riches of his grace; Wherein he hath abounded toward us in all wisdom and prudence; Having made known unto us the mystery of his will, according to his good pleasure which he hath purposed in himself: That in the dispensation of the fulness of times he might gather together in one all things in Christ, both which are in heaven, and which are on earth; even in him: In whom also we have obtained an inheritance, being predestinated according to the purpose of him who worketh all things after the counsel of his own will: That we should be to the praise of his glory, who first trusted in Christ. In whom ye also trusted, after that ye heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation: in whom also after that ye believed, ye were sealed with that holy Spirit of promise, Which is the earnest of our inheritance until the redemption of the purchased possession, unto the praise of his glory. (Eph. 1:1-14, KJV)

#### (c) Eph. 1:2, - the importance of God's grace and peace for man
> Grace be to you, and peace, from God our Father, and from the Lord Jesus Christ. (Eph. 1:2, KJV)

### (2) Eph. 1:3, - the special importance of understanding receiving all the blessings of God in Christ, accepting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ (All spiritual, mental and physical answers for the people of God)
> Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who hath blessed us with all spiritual blessings in heavenly places in Christ: (Eph. 1:3, KJV)

#### (a) The importance of accepting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, the forgiveness of all sins, justification, and adoption (salvation and being born again)

#### (b) The importance of fulfilling the destiny of God's people – The knowledge and transmission of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, the Disciples, Worldwide Evangelization

### (3) Eph. 1:4, - The importance of understanding God's sovereignty
> According as he hath chosen us in him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and without blame before him in love: (Eph. 1:4, KJV)

#### (a) God's foreknowledge and man's election (the right to choose) Ps. 139:16,Jer. 1:5,1 Pet. 1:1-2,Rom. 8:29
> Thine eyes did see my substance, yet being unperfect; and in thy book all my members were written, which in continuance were fashioned, when as yet there was none of them. (Ps. 139:16, KJV)
> Before I formed thee in the belly I knew thee; and before thou camest forth out of the womb I sanctified thee, and I ordained thee a prophet unto the nations. (Jer. 1:5, KJV)
> Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, to the strangers scattered throughout Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia, Elect according to the foreknowledge of God the Father, through sanctification of the Spirit, unto obedience and sprinkling of the blood of Jesus Christ: Grace unto you, and peace, be multiplied. (1 Pet. 1:1-2, KJV)
> For whom he did foreknow, he also did predestinate to be conformed to the image of his Son, that he might be the firstborn among many brethren. (Rom. 8:29, KJV)

#### (b) The importance of man's holiness and purity in love – acceptance of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, the importance of sanctifying God's people. (Legal and actual holiness – God's action, rebirth, sanctification, resurrection from the dead)

### (4) Eph. 1:5, - the importance of accepting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, the adoption of God's people, the fulfillment of God's will
> Having predestinated us unto the adoption of children by Jesus Christ to himself, according to the good pleasure of his will, (Eph. 1:5, KJV)

#### (a) Human salvation and adoption – God's will, God's sovereignty (foreknowledge), time schedule

#### (b) Salvation and adoption only through the acceptance of Christ – understanding Who is Christ?, the purpose of His coming? (Victim)

### (5) Eph. 1:6, - the importance of understanding God's love and grace
> To the praise of the glory of his grace, wherein he hath made us accepted in the beloved. (Eph. 1:6, KJV)

#### (a) The importance of understanding God's motives. Rom. 5:8
> But God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us. (Rom. 5:8, KJV)

#### (b) The importance of understanding the continued operation of God's grace for God's people

### (6) Eph. 1:7, - the importance of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, understanding salvation
> In whom we have redemption through his blood, the forgiveness of sins, according to the riches of his grace; (Eph. 1:7, KJV)

#### (a) The atonement of sins through the Blood of Christ (Sacrifice) is the execution of the sentence of the Word of God instead of man, the legitimate basis for the forgiveness of all sins, justification and salvation

#### (b) Forgiveness of sins – acceptance of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, understanding Who is Christ?, the purpose of His coming? Rom. 10:9
> That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved. (Rom. 10:9, KJV)

#### (c) The importance of understanding the reason for the atonement and forgiveness of the sins of God's people – God's love, grace and mercy

### (7) Eph. 1:8-10, - the importance of understanding the effect of God's grace
> Wherein he hath abounded toward us in all wisdom and prudence; Having made known unto us the mystery of his will, according to his good pleasure which he hath purposed in himself: That in the dispensation of the fulness of times he might gather together in one all things in Christ, both which are in heaven, and which are on earth; even in him: (Eph. 1:8-10, KJV)

#### (a) Understanding the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, accepting the Sacrifice of Christ – the wisdom and understanding of God. 1 Cor. 2:14
> But the natural man receiveth not the things of the Spirit of God: for they are foolishness unto him: neither can he know them, because they are spiritually discerned. (1 Cor. 2:14, KJV)

#### (b) Understanding the will of God in Christ – the knowledge and transmission of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, the Disciples, World Evangelization 1 Tim. 2:4,Matt. 28:19-20. (The importance of Communion with God, glorification and service to God)
> Who will have all men to be saved, and to come unto the knowledge of the truth. (1 Tim. 2:4, KJV)
> Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost: Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you: and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world. Amen. (Matt. 28:19-20, KJV)

#### (c) Understanding the future, the completion of God's plan – the complete return of creation to its original form (Creation)

### (8) Eph. 1:11, - the importance of understanding the heritage of God's people
> In whom also we have obtained an inheritance, being predestinated according to the purpose of him who worketh all things after the counsel of his own will: (Eph. 1:11, KJV)

#### (a) The meaning of the inheritance of God's people is Emmanuel, eternal life with God, participation in all His works in the future

#### (b) Heir status – acceptance of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ (Sacrifice)

#### (c) The status of the heir is God's foreknowledge of man (choice), His sovereign decision, and the undoubted fulfillment of God's will

### (9) Eph. 1:12, - the importance of knowing and transmitting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ
> That we should be to the praise of his glory, who first trusted in Christ. (Eph. 1:12, KJV)

#### (a) The importance of the leading generations of God's people – service to God, trust in Christ, fulfillment of destiny

#### (b) The importance of glorifying God (making Him known) is the knowledge and transmission of the Word of God. The Covenant, Christ, the Disciples, World Evangelization

### (10) Eph. 1:13-14, - The importance of understanding God's sovereignty
> In whom ye also trusted, after that ye heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation: in whom also after that ye believed, ye were sealed with that holy Spirit of promise, Which is the earnest of our inheritance until the redemption of the purchased possession, unto the praise of his glory. (Eph. 1:13-14, KJV)

#### (a) God's foreknowledge of every human being (in Him), acceptance of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, salvation, Emmanuel

#### (b) The Importance of Sealing The Holy Spirit is God's property

#### (c) The Holy Spirit is a pledge of our heritage in the future, an understanding of the stages of salvation, and the return of man to his original form. (The rebirth of the spirit and soul, a new body at the coming of Christ)

## 2) Eph. 1:15-23, - the importance of understanding the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ
> Wherefore I also, after I heard of your faith in the Lord Jesus, and love unto all the saints, Cease not to give thanks for you, making mention of you in my prayers; That the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, may give unto you the spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of him: The eyes of your understanding being enlightened; that ye may know what is the hope of his calling, and what the riches of the glory of his inheritance in the saints, And what is the exceeding greatness of his power to us-ward who believe, according to the working of his mighty power, Which he wrought in Christ, when he raised him from the dead, and set him at his own right hand in the heavenly places, Far above all principality, and power, and might, and dominion, and every name that is named, not only in this world, but also in that which is to come: And hath put all things under his feet, and gave him to be the head over all things to the church, Which is his body, the fulness of him that filleth all in all. (Eph. 1:15-23, KJV)

### (1) Eph. 1:15, - the importance of accepting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ
> Wherefore I also, after I heard of your faith in the Lord Jesus, and love unto all the saints, (Eph. 1:15, KJV)

#### (a) Acceptance of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ – the faith of God, salvation. (Correct understanding and fulfillment of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ)

#### (b) The importance of understanding and fulfilling the purpose of knowing and transmitting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, the Disciples, Worldwide Evangelization (love for all saints)

### (2) Eph. 1:16-21, - the importance of proper prayer
> Cease not to give thanks for you, making mention of you in my prayers; That the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, may give unto you the spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of him: The eyes of your understanding being enlightened; that ye may know what is the hope of his calling, and what the riches of the glory of his inheritance in the saints, And what is the exceeding greatness of his power to us-ward who believe, according to the working of his mighty power, Which he wrought in Christ, when he raised him from the dead, and set him at his own right hand in the heavenly places, Far above all principality, and power, and might, and dominion, and every name that is named, not only in this world, but also in that which is to come: (Eph. 1:16-21, KJV)

#### (a) Eph. 1:16, - the importance of giving thanks to God (salvation, Emmanuel)
> Cease not to give thanks for you, making mention of you in my prayers; (Eph. 1:16, KJV)

#### (b) Eph. 1:17, - the importance of revelation, knowledge and understanding of God and His will (Communion with God and Knowledge of God)
> That the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, may give unto you the spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of him: (Eph. 1:17, KJV)

#### (c) Eph. 1:18, - the importance of understanding the purpose of vocation and heritage, the importance of understanding and fulfilling the destiny of the people of God (Emmanuel, knowledge and transmission of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, World Evangelization)
> The eyes of your understanding being enlightened; that ye may know what is the hope of his calling, and what the riches of the glory of his inheritance in the saints, (Eph. 1:18, KJV)

#### (d) Eph. 1:19, - the importance of understanding the working of the power of God in the people of God, the salvation and preservation of man through the proclamation of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ and the fulfillment of destiny
> And what is the exceeding greatness of his power to us-ward who believe, according to the working of his mighty power, (Eph. 1:19, KJV)

#### (e) Eph. 1:19, - the importance of understanding the reason for the faith of God's people (the effect of God's grace). John 15:5
> And what is the exceeding greatness of his power to us-ward who believe, according to the working of his mighty power, (Eph. 1:19, KJV)
> I am the vine, ye are the branches: He that abideth in me, and I in him, the same bringeth forth much fruit: for without me ye can do nothing. (John 15:5, KJV)

#### (e) Eph. 1:20-21, - the resurrection and ascension of Jesus Christ, the action of God's grace, the fulfillment of God's will and plan
> Which he wrought in Christ, when he raised him from the dead, and set him at his own right hand in the heavenly places, Far above all principality, and power, and might, and dominion, and every name that is named, not only in this world, but also in that which is to come: (Eph. 1:20-21, KJV)

### (3) Eph. 1:22-23, - the importance of understanding the Church of Christ
> And hath put all things under his feet, and gave him to be the head over all things to the church, Which is his body, the fulness of him that filleth all in all. (Eph. 1:22-23, KJV)

#### (a) Jesus Christ is the head of the Church

#### (b) The Church is the body of Jesus Christ, all the saved people
