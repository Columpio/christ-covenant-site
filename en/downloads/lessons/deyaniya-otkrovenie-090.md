## 2) Phil. 1:3-11, - the importance of fulfilling the purpose
> I thank my God upon every remembrance of you, Always in every prayer of mine for you all making request with joy, For your fellowship in the gospel from the first day until now; Being confident of this very thing, that he which hath begun a good work in you will perform it until the day of Jesus Christ: Even as it is meet for me to think this of you all, because I have you in my heart; inasmuch as both in my bonds, and in the defence and confirmation of the gospel, ye all are partakers of my grace. For God is my record, how greatly I long after you all in the bowels of Jesus Christ. And this I pray, that your love may abound yet more and more in knowledge and in all judgment; That ye may approve things that are excellent; that ye may be sincere and without offence till the day of Christ; Being filled with the fruits of righteousness, which are by Jesus Christ, unto the glory and praise of God. (Phil. 1:3-11, KJV)

### (1) Phil. 1:3-5, - the importance of prayer for the people of God, the importance of fulfilling one's destiny (evangelism)
> I thank my God upon every remembrance of you, Always in every prayer of mine for you all making request with joy, For your fellowship in the gospel from the first day until now; (Phil. 1:3-5, KJV)

### (2) Phil. 1:6, - the importance of understanding the operation of God's grace – the knowledge and transmission of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ (good deed), the accomplishment of right actions in the life of every human being
> Being confident of this very thing, that he which hath begun a good work in you will perform it until the day of Jesus Christ: (Phil. 1:6, KJV)

### (3) Phil. 1:7, - the importance of thinking correctly and understanding the purpose of the life of God's people (partners in the cause of God)
> Even as it is meet for me to think this of you all, because I have you in my heart; inasmuch as both in my bonds, and in the defence and confirmation of the gospel, ye all are partakers of my grace. (Phil. 1:7, KJV)

### (4) Phil. 1:8, - the importance of properly understanding the love of God, the knowledge and transmission of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, the importance of human salvation, responsibility and caring
> For God is my record, how greatly I long after you all in the bowels of Jesus Christ. (Phil. 1:8, KJV)

### (5) Phil. 1:9-10, - the importance of growing in the love of God, knowledge of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, fulfillment of destiny, being pure and unblemished (deep faith of God)
> And this I pray, that your love may abound yet more and more in knowledge and in all judgment; That ye may approve things that are excellent; that ye may be sincere and without offence till the day of Christ; (Phil. 1:9-10, KJV)

### (6) Phil. 1:11, - the importance of the fruits of righteousness, the consequence of accepting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, the right life of God's children
> Being filled with the fruits of righteousness, which are by Jesus Christ, unto the glory and praise of God. (Phil. 1:11, KJV)

## 3) Phil. 1:12-19, - the importance of knowing and transmitting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ
> But I would ye should understand, brethren, that the things which happened unto me have fallen out rather unto the furtherance of the gospel; So that my bonds in Christ are manifest in all the palace, and in all other places; And many of the brethren in the Lord, waxing confident by my bonds, are much more bold to speak the word without fear. Some indeed preach Christ even of envy and strife; and some also of good will: The one preach Christ of contention, not sincerely, supposing to add affliction to my bonds: But the other of love, knowing that I am set for the defence of the gospel. What then? notwithstanding, every way, whether in pretence, or in truth, Christ is preached; and I therein do rejoice, yea, and will rejoice. For I know that this shall turn to my salvation through your prayer, and the supply of the Spirit of Jesus Christ, (Phil. 1:12-19, KJV)

### (1) Phil. 1:12-14, - the importance of fulfilling destiny, God's control of circumstances (all for the good). Rom. 8:28
> But I would ye should understand, brethren, that the things which happened unto me have fallen out rather unto the furtherance of the gospel; So that my bonds in Christ are manifest in all the palace, and in all other places; And many of the brethren in the Lord, waxing confident by my bonds, are much more bold to speak the word without fear. (Phil. 1:12-14, KJV)
> And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose. (Rom. 8:28, KJV)

### (2) Phil. 1:14, - the importance of a personal example of fulfilling one's destiny, fulfilling God's will in any situation
> And many of the brethren in the Lord, waxing confident by my bonds, are much more bold to speak the word without fear. (Phil. 1:14, KJV)

### (3) Phil. 1:15-17, - the importance of correct preaching motives
> Some indeed preach Christ even of envy and strife; and some also of good will: The one preach Christ of contention, not sincerely, supposing to add affliction to my bonds: But the other of love, knowing that I am set for the defence of the gospel. (Phil. 1:15-17, KJV)

#### (a) Envy and inquisitiveness, there is no purity of teaching – the desire to stand out, human ambitions (an attempt to compare with Paul)

#### (b) Love is the affirmation of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, the purity of teaching, Paul's support in defending the Truth of God

### (4) Phil. 1:18, - the importance of preaching about Christ, the opportunity to delve into the Word of God, gaining understanding from God
> What then? notwithstanding, every way, whether in pretence, or in truth, Christ is preached; and I therein do rejoice, yea, and will rejoice. (Phil. 1:18, KJV)

#### (a) The problem of the religions of the world is the withdrawal of man from Christ

#### (b) Incorrect Christianity (mixed gospel) is a guide to Christ for every person. Gal. 3:24
> Wherefore the law was our schoolmaster to bring us unto Christ, that we might be justified by faith. (Gal. 3:24, KJV)

### (5) Phil. 1:19, - the importance of fulfilling the destiny of the people of God
> For I know that this shall turn to my salvation through your prayer, and the supply of the Spirit of Jesus Christ, (Phil. 1:19, KJV)

#### (a) Preaching about Christ – Paul's confession and choice

#### (b) The prayer of God's people – Paul's support, the importance of preserving God's faith until the moment of separation of soul and body

#### (c) The cooperation of the Spirit of Jesus Christ is an action of God's grace

## 4) Phil. 1:20-26, - the importance of knowing and transmitting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ
> According to my earnest expectation and my hope, that in nothing I shall be ashamed, but that with all boldness, as always, so now also Christ shall be magnified in my body, whether it be by life, or by death. For to me to live is Christ, and to die is gain. But if I live in the flesh, this is the fruit of my labour: yet what I shall choose I wot not. For I am in a strait betwixt two, having a desire to depart, and to be with Christ; which is far better: Nevertheless to abide in the flesh is more needful for you. And having this confidence, I know that I shall abide and continue with you all for your furtherance and joy of faith; That your rejoicing may be more abundant in Jesus Christ for me by my coming to you again. (Phil. 1:20-26, KJV)

### (1) Phil. 1:20, - the importance of glorifying and exalting Christ (by life or death), the destiny of the people of God
> According to my earnest expectation and my hope, that in nothing I shall be ashamed, but that with all boldness, as always, so now also Christ shall be magnified in my body, whether it be by life, or by death. (Phil. 1:20, KJV)

### (2) Phil. 1:21-26, - the importance of confidence in the forgiveness of all sins and salvation (confession of the Apostle Paul)
> For to me to live is Christ, and to die is gain. But if I live in the flesh, this is the fruit of my labour: yet what I shall choose I wot not. For I am in a strait betwixt two, having a desire to depart, and to be with Christ; which is far better: Nevertheless to abide in the flesh is more needful for you. And having this confidence, I know that I shall abide and continue with you all for your furtherance and joy of faith; That your rejoicing may be more abundant in Jesus Christ for me by my coming to you again. (Phil. 1:21-26, KJV)

#### (a) Life is Christ (Emmanuel, fulfillment of God's will)

#### (b) Death is an acquisition (Kingdom of Heaven)

#### (c) To resolve oneself and be with Christ – Paul's assurance

#### (d) Being in the flesh is more necessary for believers – an understanding and desire to fulfill their destiny, to serve people

## 5) Phil. 1:27-30, - the importance of knowing and transmitting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ
> Only let your conversation be as it becometh the gospel of Christ: that whether I come and see you, or else be absent, I may hear of your affairs, that ye stand fast in one spirit, with one mind striving together for the faith of the gospel; And in nothing terrified by your adversaries: which is to them an evident token of perdition, but to you of salvation, and that of God. For unto you it is given in the behalf of Christ, not only to believe on him, but also to suffer for his sake; Having the same conflict which ye saw in me, and now hear to be in me. (Phil. 1:27-30, KJV)

### (1) Phil. 1:27, - the importance of the right life of the people of God, the importance of fulfilling the destiny
> Only let your conversation be as it becometh the gospel of Christ: that whether I come and see you, or else be absent, I may hear of your affairs, that ye stand fast in one spirit, with one mind striving together for the faith of the gospel; (Phil. 1:27, KJV)

### (2) Phil. 1:28-30, - the importance of fulfilling the purpose, the importance of the right attitude to circumstances
> And in nothing terrified by your adversaries: which is to them an evident token of perdition, but to you of salvation, and that of God. For unto you it is given in the behalf of Christ, not only to believe on him, but also to suffer for his sake; Having the same conflict which ye saw in me, and now hear to be in me. (Phil. 1:28-30, KJV)

#### (a) The sufferings of God's people are the glorification of Christ, the understanding of salvation and the life of the next age

#### (b) The sufferings of the opponents of God's faith are a type and an omen of destruction (Lake of fire)

#### (c) Paul's example is the importance of knowing and transmitting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, the whole life work of God's people
