## 2) Col. 1:9-23, - affirmation of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ
> For this cause we also, since the day we heard it, do not cease to pray for you, and to desire that ye might be filled with the knowledge of his will in all wisdom and spiritual understanding; That ye might walk worthy of the Lord unto all pleasing, being fruitful in every good work, and increasing in the knowledge of God; Strengthened with all might, according to his glorious power, unto all patience and longsuffering with joyfulness; Giving thanks unto the Father, which hath made us meet to be partakers of the inheritance of the saints in light: Who hath delivered us from the power of darkness, and hath translated us into the kingdom of his dear Son: In whom we have redemption through his blood, even the forgiveness of sins: Who is the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of every creature: For by him were all things created, that are in heaven, and that are in earth, visible and invisible, whether they be thrones, or dominions, or principalities, or powers: all things were created by him, and for him: And he is before all things, and by him all things consist. And he is the head of the body, the church: who is the beginning, the firstborn from the dead; that in all things he might have the preeminence. For it pleased the Father that in him should all fulness dwell; And, having made peace through the blood of his cross, by him to reconcile all things unto himself; by him, I say, whether they be things in earth, or things in heaven. And you, that were sometime alienated and enemies in your mind by wicked works, yet now hath he reconciled In the body of his flesh through death, to present you holy and unblameable and unreproveable in his sight: If ye continue in the faith grounded and settled, and be not moved away from the hope of the gospel, which ye have heard, and which was preached to every creature which is under heaven; whereof I Paul am made a minister; (Col. 1:9-23, KJV)

> Who is the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of every creature: For by him were all things created, that are in heaven, and that are in earth, visible and invisible, whether they be thrones, or dominions, or principalities, or powers: all things were created by him, and for him: And he is before all things, and by him all things consist. > Who is the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of every creature: (Col. 1:15, KJV)

#### (c) Col. 1:17, - the importance of understanding the reason for the existence and preservation of the spiritual and physical world (the grace of Christ)
> And he is before all things, and by him all things consist. (Col. 1:17, KJV)

#### (d) Col. 1:18, - Jesus Christ is the head of the Church, the importance of understanding God's sovereignty and control

#### (e) Col. 1:18, - Jesus Christ is the firstborn from the dead, the importance of the resurrection of Christ (God in the flesh, fulfillment of the Word of God), confidence in salvation, understanding of the future

#### (e) Col. 1:19, - Jesus Christ is the fullness of creation, the importance of knowing the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, all the answers

#### (g) Col. 1:20, - Jesus Christ is God's only way of reconciliation for man through His Sacrifice and Blood
> And, having made peace through the blood of his cross, by him to reconcile all things unto himself; by him, I say, whether they be things in earth, or things in heaven. (Col. 1:20, KJV)

## 3) Col. 1:21-23, - the importance of understanding the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ
> And you, that were sometime alienated and enemies in your mind by wicked works, yet now hath he reconciled In the body of his flesh through death, to present you holy and unblameable and unreproveable in his sight: If ye continue in the faith grounded and settled, and be not moved away from the hope of the gospel, which ye have heard, and which was preached to every creature which is under heaven; whereof I Paul am made a minister; (Col. 1:21-23, KJV)

### (1) Col. 1:21, - The importance of understanding a spiritual problem
> And you, that were sometime alienated and enemies in your mind by wicked works, yet now hath he reconciled (Col. 1:21, KJV)

#### (a) Alienated from God – the power of sin, death and hell, Satan

#### (b) Enemies – resisting God and His Word, children of the devil

#### (c) Disposed to evil deeds – the sinful nature of man

### (2) Col. 1:22, - the importance of understanding God's decision
> In the body of his flesh through death, to present you holy and unblameable and unreproveable in his sight: (Col. 1:22, KJV)

#### (a) Reconciliation with God through the Sacrifice of Christ - forgiveness of all human sins, salvation

#### (b) The holiness of man – Christ (God's view of man)

#### (c) The purity of man – Christ (God's view of man)

#### (d) Innocence (justification) – Christ

### (3) Col. 1:23, - the importance of understanding purpose
> If ye continue in the faith grounded and settled, and be not moved away from the hope of the gospel, which ye have heard, and which was preached to every creature which is under heaven; whereof I Paul am made a minister; (Col. 1:23, KJV)

#### (a) The importance of maintaining the faith of God until the end of life, the knowledge and transmission of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ – God's conditions. (If you remain in faith and do not fall away from the gospel)

#### (b) The importance of proclaiming the Word of God, the Covenant, the Christ to all creatures in the heavenly kingdom, the importance of accomplishing World Evangelization (purpose)

#### (c) The importance of ministers (disciples of Christ) – the knowledge and transmission of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ

## 4) Col. 1:24-29, - the importance of knowing and transmitting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ

#### (a) Emmanuel is the Christ in us (the principle of Creation)

#### (b) Salvation and adoption of the Gentiles through evangelism, understanding of God's love and mercy. Eph. 3:3-6,Rom. 2:11

> For there is no respect of persons with God. (Rom. 2:11, KJV)

#### (b) Bringing a person to perfection in Christ (teaching)

#### (c) The importance of doing work in the field of God – the knowledge and transmission of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, the disciples, World Evangelization

#### (d) The importance of striving by the power of God – the importance of fulfilling one's destiny and trusting in God
