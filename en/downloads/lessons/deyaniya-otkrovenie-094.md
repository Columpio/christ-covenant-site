# Col. 2
## 2.1) Col. 2:1-11, - the importance of knowing and transmitting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ
> For I would that ye knew what great conflict I have for you, and for them at Laodicea, and for as many as have not seen my face in the flesh; That their hearts might be comforted, being knit together in love, and unto all riches of the full assurance of understanding, to the acknowledgement of the mystery of God, and of the Father, and of Christ; In whom are hid all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge. And this I say, lest any man should beguile you with enticing words. For though I be absent in the flesh, yet am I with you in the spirit, joying and beholding your order, and the stedfastness of your faith in Christ. As ye have therefore received Christ Jesus the Lord, so walk ye in him: Rooted and built up in him, and stablished in the faith, as ye have been taught, abounding therein with thanksgiving. Beware lest any man spoil you through philosophy and vain deceit, after the tradition of men, after the rudiments of the world, and not after Christ. For in him dwelleth all the fulness of the Godhead bodily. And ye are complete in him, which is the head of all principality and power: In whom also ye are circumcised with the circumcision made without hands, in putting off the body of the sins of the flesh by the circumcision of Christ: (Col. 2:1-11, KJV)

## 2) Col. 2:11-15, - the importance of understanding the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ
> In whom also ye are circumcised with the circumcision made without hands, in putting off the body of the sins of the flesh by the circumcision of Christ: Buried with him in baptism, wherein also ye are risen with him through the faith of the operation of God, who hath raised him from the dead. And you, being dead in your sins and the uncircumcision of your flesh, hath he quickened together with him, having forgiven you all trespasses; Blotting out the handwriting of ordinances that was against us, which was contrary to us, and took it out of the way, nailing it to his cross; And having spoiled principalities and powers, he made a shew of them openly, triumphing over them in it. (Col. 2:11-15, KJV)

### (1) Col. 2:11, - the importance of understanding N.Z. circumcision, Water Baptism, visible confirmation of entering into a Covenant with God
> In whom also ye are circumcised with the circumcision made without hands, in putting off the body of the sins of the flesh by the circumcision of Christ: (Col. 2:11, KJV)

### (2) Col. 2:12, - the importance of understanding the death of the old man (putting off the sinful body of the flesh, burial with Christ) and understanding the resurrection of the new man (being born again, being raised up with Christ through the faith of God), the salvation of man
> Buried with him in baptism, wherein also ye are risen with him through the faith of the operation of God, who hath raised him from the dead. (Col. 2:12, KJV)

### (3) Col. 2:13, - the importance of understanding the spiritual problem of man (dead in sins and uncircumcision of the flesh), old man, sinner, lawful and eternal separation from God
> And you, being dead in your sins and the uncircumcision of your flesh, hath he quickened together with him, having forgiven you all trespasses; (Col. 2:13, KJV)

### (4) Col. 2:13, - the importance of understanding the solution of a spiritual problem, the sacrifice of Christ, the forgiveness of all sins (past, present, future), being born again (the quickening of man, the action of God)
> And you, being dead in your sins and the uncircumcision of your flesh, hath he quickened together with him, having forgiven you all trespasses; (Col. 2:13, KJV)

### (5) Col. 2:14, - the importance of fulfilling the Commandments of the Law of God (requirements, justice of the Law, testimony against us), the execution of the sentence of the Word of God instead of man. Matt. 5:17,Rom. 10:4
> Blotting out the handwriting of ordinances that was against us, which was contrary to us, and took it out of the way, nailing it to his cross; (Col. 2:14, KJV)
> Think not that I am come to destroy the law, or the prophets: I am not come to destroy, but to fulfil. (Matt. 5:17, KJV)
> For Christ is the end of the law for righteousness to every one that believeth. (Rom. 10:4, KJV)

### (6) Col. 2:15, - the execution of the sentence of the Word of God instead of man, the solution of the problem of sin and its consequences, the crushing of the power of Satan and demons (spiritual authorities and authorities)
> And having spoiled principalities and powers, he made a shew of them openly, triumphing over them in it. (Col. 2:15, KJV)

## 3) Col. 2:16-23, - the importance of understanding the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ
> Let no man therefore judge you in meat, or in drink, or in respect of an holyday, or of the new moon, or of the sabbath days: Which are a shadow of things to come; but the body is of Christ. Let no man beguile you of your reward in a voluntary humility and worshipping of angels, intruding into those things which he hath not seen, vainly puffed up by his fleshly mind, And not holding the Head, from which all the body by joints and bands having nourishment ministered, and knit together, increaseth with the increase of God. Wherefore if ye be dead with Christ from the rudiments of the world, why, as though living in the world, are ye subject to ordinances, (Touch not; taste not; handle not; Which all are to perish with the using;) after the commandments and doctrines of men? Which things have indeed a shew of wisdom in will worship, and humility, and neglecting of the body; not in any honour to the satisfying of the flesh. (Col. 2:16-23, KJV)

### (1) Col. 2:16, - the importance of understanding the Sacrifice of Christ, the full fulfillment of the Law instead of man, the inadmissibility of a mixed gospel, falling away from Christ and the grace of God. (Adding works in the Sacrifice of Christ as a condition for salvation) Gal. 5:4
> Let no man therefore judge you in meat, or in drink, or in respect of an holyday, or of the new moon, or of the sabbath days: (Col. 2:16, KJV)
> Christ is become of no effect unto you, whosoever of you are justified by the law; ye are fallen from grace. (Gal. 5:4, KJV)

### (2) Col. 2:17, - The Law of V.Z.'s Commandments pointed to Christ (the shadow of the future), the full fulfillment of the Law only by Christ (the impossibility of fully fulfilling the Law for man)
> Which are a shadow of things to come; but the body is of Christ. (Col. 2:17, KJV)

### (3) Col. 2:17, - the importance of understanding spiritual reality, the action of N.Z. (The body, the Church – in Christ, everything is accomplished)
> Which are a shadow of things to come; but the body is of Christ. (Col. 2:17, KJV)

### (4) Col. 2:18, - the importance of understanding the work of Satan, the false teaching. (The teachings about Angels as intermediaries between man and God, the intrusion into what they do not see and do not know is a senseless arrogance of the carnal mind of man). 1 Cor. 2:4
> Let no man beguile you of your reward in a voluntary humility and worshipping of angels, intruding into those things which he hath not seen, vainly puffed up by his fleshly mind, (Col. 2:18, KJV)
> And my speech and my preaching was not with enticing words of man’s wisdom, but in demonstration of the Spirit and of power: (1 Cor. 2:4, KJV)

### (5) Col. 2:19, is the abandonment of trust in Christ as the Sole Mediator of God's people, the Head of the Church, and the union of God's children into one by the action of God's grace. 1 Tim. 2:5
> And not holding the Head, from which all the body by joints and bands having nourishment ministered, and knit together, increaseth with the increase of God. (Col. 2:19, KJV)
> For there is one God, and one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus; (1 Tim. 2:5, KJV)

### (6) Col. 2:20-22, - the importance of fulfilling the destiny of God's people, the importance of rejecting false teachings. (The teaching of Gnosticism, neglect of the physical body – asceticism or permissiveness, Nicolaitanism)
> Wherefore if ye be dead with Christ from the rudiments of the world, why, as though living in the world, are ye subject to ordinances, (Touch not; taste not; handle not; Which all are to perish with the using;) after the commandments and doctrines of men? (Col. 2:20-22, KJV)
