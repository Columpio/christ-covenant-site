### (2) 1 Thess. 4:2, - the importance of understanding God's commandments
> For ye know what commandments we gave you by the Lord Jesus. (1 Thess. 4:2, KJV)

#### (a) The commandment of the Covenant is the acceptance of Jesus of Nazareth as the Messiah, Christ (God in the flesh), the acceptance of the forgiveness of all sins through the Sacrifice of Christ (justification and salvation of the people of God)

#### (b) The commandment of the Law is the understanding of sin and the impossibility of salvation by human forces, the right life and service to God

#### (c) To love the Lord God and one's neighbor as oneself – the importance of fulfilling the commandments of the Covenant and the Law (try)
