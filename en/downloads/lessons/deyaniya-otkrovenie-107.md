# Heb. 1–2
## 1.1) Heb. 1, - the importance of understanding the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ

### (1) Heb. 1:1-2, - the importance of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, understanding the will of God, the importance of understanding Christ for every human being Gen. 1:27; 2:7, 9, 16-17, - Gen. 3:1-7 (Rom. 5:12; 6:23,Eph. 2:2), -Gen. 3:15, 21,Matt. 16:16 (1 Tim. 3:16), John 19:30, - Rom. 10:9
> God, who at sundry times and in divers manners spake in time past unto the fathers by the prophets, Hath in these last days spoken unto us by his Son, whom he hath appointed heir of all things, by whom also he made the worlds; (Heb. 1:1-2, KJV)
> So God created man in his own image, in the image of God created he him; male and female created he them. (Gen. 1:27, KJV)
> And the LORD God formed man of the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life; and man became a living soul. (Gen. 2:7, KJV)
> And out of the ground made the LORD God to grow every tree that is pleasant to the sight, and good for food; the tree of life also in the midst of the garden, and the tree of knowledge of good and evil. (Gen. 2:9, KJV)
> And the LORD God commanded the man, saying, Of every tree of the garden thou mayest freely eat: But of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, thou shalt not eat of it: for in the day that thou eatest thereof thou shalt surely die. (Gen. 2:16-17, KJV)
> Now the serpent was more subtil than any beast of the field which the LORD God had made. And he said unto the woman, Yea, hath God said, Ye shall not eat of every tree of the garden? And the woman said unto the serpent, We may eat of the fruit of the trees of the garden: But of the fruit of the tree which is in the midst of the garden, God hath said, Ye shall not eat of it, neither shall ye touch it, lest ye die. And the serpent said unto the woman, Ye shall not surely die: For God doth know that in the day ye eat thereof, then your eyes shall be opened, and ye shall be as gods, knowing good and evil. And when the woman saw that the tree was good for food, and that it was pleasant to the eyes, and a tree to be desired to make one wise, she took of the fruit thereof, and did eat, and gave also unto her husband with her; and he did eat. And the eyes of them both were opened, and they knew that they were naked; and they sewed fig leaves together, and made themselves aprons. (Gen. 3:1-7, KJV)
> Wherefore, as by one man sin entered into the world, and death by sin; and so death passed upon all men, for that all have sinned: (Rom. 5:12, KJV)
> For the wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord. (Rom. 6:23, KJV)
> Wherein in time past ye walked according to the course of this world, according to the prince of the power of the air, the spirit that now worketh in the children of disobedience: (Eph. 2:2, KJV)
> And I will put enmity between thee and the woman, and between thy seed and her seed; it shall bruise thy head, and thou shalt bruise his heel. (Gen. 3:15, KJV)
> Unto Adam also and to his wife did the LORD God make coats of skins, and clothed them. (Gen. 3:21, KJV)
> And Simon Peter answered and said, Thou art the Christ, the Son of the living God. (Matt. 16:16, KJV)
> And without controversy great is the mystery of godliness: God was manifest in the flesh, justified in the Spirit, seen of angels, preached unto the Gentiles, believed on in the world, received up into glory. (1 Tim. 3:16, KJV)
> When Jesus therefore had received the vinegar, he said, It is finished: and he bowed his head, and gave up the ghost. (John 19:30, KJV)
> That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved. (Rom. 10:9, KJV)

### (2) Heb. 1:2-3, - the importance of understanding Christ for man
> Hath in these last days spoken unto us by his Son, whom he hath appointed heir of all things, by whom also he made the worlds; Who being the brightness of his glory, and the express image of his person, and upholding all things by the word of his power, when he had by himself purged our sins, sat down on the right hand of the Majesty on high; (Heb. 1:2-3, KJV)

#### (a) The creation of the visible and invisible world through the Lord Jesus Christ, the understanding of the Divinity of Jesus Christ

#### (b) The radiance of glory and the image of the hypostasis of God – the image of the invisible God, the fullness of the Godhead bodily

#### (c) He holds everything by the Word of His power – the King of Kings and Lord of Lords, God's sovereignty and control

#### (d) He has cleansed our sins by Himself – the importance of understanding the Sacrifice of Christ, solving the problem of all the sins of mankind (past, present and future)

#### (e) He sat at the right hand of the throne of majesty on high – the importance of understanding the resurrection and ascension of Christ, the ministry of Christ as the only Mediator, High Priest and Intercessor

### (3) Heb. 1:4-14, - the importance of understanding the nature of Christ, God in the flesh, the Only Begotten Son of God, the Lord of all. (The problem of understanding the nature and status of Angels and Christ)
> Being made so much better than the angels, as he hath by inheritance obtained a more excellent name than they. For unto which of the angels said he at any time, Thou art my Son, this day have I begotten thee? And again, I will be to him a Father, and he shall be to me a Son? And again, when he bringeth in the firstbegotten into the world, he saith, And let all the angels of God worship him. And of the angels he saith, Who maketh his angels spirits, and his ministers a flame of fire. But unto the Son he saith, Thy throne, O God, is for ever and ever: a sceptre of righteousness is the sceptre of thy kingdom. Thou hast loved righteousness, and hated iniquity; therefore God, even thy God, hath anointed thee with the oil of gladness above thy fellows. And, Thou, Lord, in the beginning hast laid the foundation of the earth; and the heavens are the works of thine hands: They shall perish; but thou remainest; and they all shall wax old as doth a garment; And as a vesture shalt thou fold them up, and they shall be changed: but thou art the same, and thy years shall not fail. But to which of the angels said he at any time, Sit on my right hand, until I make thine enemies thy footstool? Are they not all ministering spirits, sent forth to minister for them who shall be heirs of salvation? (Heb. 1:4-14, KJV)

#### (a) Heb. 1:4, is more excellent than the Angels, the most glorious Name
> Being made so much better than the angels, as he hath by inheritance obtained a more excellent name than they. (Heb. 1:4, KJV)

#### (b) Heb. 1:5, - Angels, creatures of God, Christ, born of God, the Only Begotten Son of God (You are My Son)
> For unto which of the angels said he at any time, Thou art my Son, this day have I begotten thee? And again, I will be to him a Father, and he shall be to me a Son? (Heb. 1:5, KJV)

#### (c) Heb. 1:6, - the worship of all Angels to Christ, the Divinity of Christ and His dominion over the spiritual and physical world
> And again, when he bringeth in the firstbegotten into the world, he saith, And let all the angels of God worship him. (Heb. 1:6, KJV)

#### (d) Heb. 1:7, - Angels, servants of God (serving Christ)
> And of the angels he saith, Who maketh his angels spirits, and his ministers a flame of fire. (Heb. 1:7, KJV)

#### (e) Heb. 1:8, - the eternal kingdom and reign of Jesus Christ
> But unto the Son he saith, Thy throne, O God, is for ever and ever: a sceptre of righteousness is the sceptre of thy kingdom. (Heb. 1:8, KJV)

#### (e) Heb. 1:9, - Jesus Christ loved righteousness and hated lawlessness, the affirmation of God's Holiness and Righteousness
> Thou hast loved righteousness, and hated iniquity; therefore God, even thy God, hath anointed thee with the oil of gladness above thy fellows. (Heb. 1:9, KJV)

#### (g) Heb. 1:10-12, - the eternity and immutability of Christ, God in the flesh
> And, Thou, Lord, in the beginning hast laid the foundation of the earth; and the heavens are the works of thine hands: They shall perish; but thou remainest; and they all shall wax old as doth a garment; And as a vesture shalt thou fold them up, and they shall be changed: but thou art the same, and thy years shall not fail. (Heb. 1:10-12, KJV)

#### (h) Heb. 1:13, - the place of Christ at the right hand of God (equality with God)
> But to which of the angels said he at any time, Sit on my right hand, until I make thine enemies thy footstool? (Heb. 1:13, KJV)

#### (i) Heb. 1:14, - Angels, ministering spirits of the children of God
> Are they not all ministering spirits, sent forth to minister for them who shall be heirs of salvation? (Heb. 1:14, KJV)

## 2) Heb. 2, - the importance of knowing and transmitting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ

### (1) Heb. 2:1, - the importance of the Word of God, of the Covenant, of Christ for every human being
> Therefore we ought to give the more earnest heed to the things which we have heard, lest at any time we should let them slip. (Heb. 2:1, KJV)

#### (a) The importance of hearing the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ

#### (b) The importance of understanding the possibility of falling away from God

### (2) Heb. 2:2-4, - the importance of understanding the action of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ
> For if the word spoken by angels was stedfast, and every transgression and disobedience received a just recompence of reward; How shall we escape, if we neglect so great salvation; which at the first began to be spoken by the Lord, and was confirmed unto us by them that heard him; God also bearing them witness, both with signs and wonders, and with divers miracles, and gifts of the Holy Ghost, according to his own will? (Heb. 2:2-4, KJV)

#### (a) Heb. 2:2, - the importance of understanding V.Z., the action of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ
> For if the word spoken by angels was stedfast, and every transgression and disobedience received a just recompence of reward; (Heb. 2:2, KJV)

#### (b) Heb. 2:3-4, - the importance of understanding N.Z. (the preaching of Christ), the unchanging effect of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ (testimonies from God)
> How shall we escape, if we neglect so great salvation; which at the first began to be spoken by the Lord, and was confirmed unto us by them that heard him; God also bearing them witness, both with signs and wonders, and with divers miracles, and gifts of the Holy Ghost, according to his own will? (Heb. 2:3-4, KJV)

### (3) Heb. 2:5-8, - the importance of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ for man, the importance of understanding the will of God for man, the importance of understanding the principle of Creation (conquered everything) and the principle of the Fall (the fall of man, now not everything is conquered)
> For unto the angels hath he not put in subjection the world to come, whereof we speak. But one in a certain place testified, saying, What is man, that thou art mindful of him? or the son of man, that thou visitest him? Thou madest him a little lower than the angels; thou crownedst him with glory and honour, and didst set him over the works of thy hands: Thou hast put all things in subjection under his feet. For in that he put all in subjection under him, he left nothing that is not put under him. But now we see not yet all things put under him. (Heb. 2:5-8, KJV)

### (4) Heb. 2:9-10, - the importance of understanding Restoration, the Sacrifice and Labor of Christ, understanding the will of God. 1 Tim. 2:4
> But we see Jesus, who was made a little lower than the angels for the suffering of death, crowned with glory and honour; that he by the grace of God should taste death for every man. For it became him, for whom are all things, and by whom are all things, in bringing many sons unto glory, to make the captain of their salvation perfect through sufferings. (Heb. 2:9-10, KJV)
> Who will have all men to be saved, and to come unto the knowledge of the truth. (1 Tim. 2:4, KJV)

#### (a) Suffering death is the Sacrifice of Christ, God's plan

#### (b) He was humiliated a little before the Angels – the likeness of sinful flesh, the importance of understanding the incarnation of Christ. Phil. 2:7. (Became a slave in appearance, suffered suffering and death for people)
> But made himself of no reputation, and took upon him the form of a servant, and was made in the likeness of men: (Phil. 2:7, KJV)

#### (c) By the grace of God, he tasted death for all – the importance of a deep understanding of the Sacrifice and Heroic Deed of Jesus Christ

#### (d) The One for whom everything is and from Whom everything is (Christ), who leads many sons to glory (children of God), the Leader of salvation, who is perfect through suffering (Christ's sacrifice)

### (5) Heb. 2:11-13, - The Sanctifier (Christ) and the sanctified (God's children) are all from One (the Triune God), calls the people of God His brothers (God's love and mercy, adoption)
> For both he that sanctifieth and they who are sanctified are all of one: for which cause he is not ashamed to call them brethren, Saying, I will declare thy name unto my brethren, in the midst of the church will I sing praise unto thee. And again, I will put my trust in him. And again, Behold I and the children which God hath given me. (Heb. 2:11-13, KJV)

### (6) Heb. 2:14, - the importance of understanding the incarnation of Christ, accepting the likeness of sinful flesh, the opportunity to die and rise again for the sins of the whole world (fulfilling the sentence of the Word of God)
> Forasmuch then as the children are partakers of flesh and blood, he also himself likewise took part of the same; that through death he might destroy him that had the power of death, that is, the devil; (Heb. 2:14, KJV)

### (7) Heb. 2:14, - through death to deprive those who have the power of death of their power, the importance of understanding the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, the importance of solving the problem of sin (the power of death and hell, Satan)
> Forasmuch then as the children are partakers of flesh and blood, he also himself likewise took part of the same; that through death he might destroy him that had the power of death, that is, the devil; (Heb. 2:14, KJV)

### (8) Heb. 2:15, - the importance of understanding the reason for the bondage of sin, death and hell, Satan, the importance of solving the spiritual problem of man
> And deliver them who through fear of death were all their lifetime subject to bondage. (Heb. 2:15, KJV)

### (9) Heb. 2:16, - the importance of understanding the value and status of God's children, understanding the true descendants of Abraham. Rom. 9:6-8
> For verily he took not on him the nature of angels; but he took on him the seed of Abraham. (Heb. 2:16, KJV)
> Not as though the word of God hath taken none effect. For they are not all Israel, which are of Israel: Neither, because they are the seed of Abraham, are they all children: but, In Isaac shall thy seed be called. That is, They which are the children of the flesh, these are not the children of God: but the children of the promise are counted for the seed. (Rom. 9:6-8, KJV)

#### (a) The true descendants of Abraham are the acceptance of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ

#### (b) The false descendants of Abraham – the rejection of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ

### (10) Heb. 2:17-18, - the importance of understanding the incarnation and Sacrifice of Christ (he was tempted, endured everything, died and rose again), the legitimate right to be a High Priest and Helper for all mankind (God's boundless love and mercy)
> Wherefore in all things it behoved him to be made like unto his brethren, that he might be a merciful and faithful high priest in things pertaining to God, to make reconciliation for the sins of the people. For in that he himself hath suffered being tempted, he is able to succour them that are tempted. (Heb. 2:17-18, KJV)
