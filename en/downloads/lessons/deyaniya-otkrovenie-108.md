# Heb. 3–4

#### (b) The importance of understanding the Messenger and High Priest of our confession – understanding Christ and His work

#### (c) The importance of confessing to the people of God – proclaiming the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, the importance of living in the faith of God

### (2) Heb. 3:2-6, - the importance of understanding the superiority of Christ over Moses (the problem of the Jews' view of Moses)
> Who was faithful to him that appointed him, as also Moses was faithful in all his house. For this man was counted worthy of more glory than Moses, inasmuch as he who hath builded the house hath more honour than the house. For every house is builded by some man; but he that built all things is God. And Moses verily was faithful in all his house, as a servant, for a testimony of those things which were to be spoken after; But Christ as a son over his own house; whose house are we, if we hold fast the confidence and the rejoicing of the hope firm unto the end. (Heb. 3:2-6, KJV)

#### (a) The House of God is the people of God

#### (b) The Creator of the house of God is the Holy, Triune God

#### (c) Moses is a servant in the house of God (a man) – a proclamation of the future, an indication of V.Z. to the coming Christ, the salvation of man

#### (d) Christ is the Son in the house of God, the Master of the house, the Lord and God

## 2) Heb. 4, - the importance of knowing and transmitting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ

### (1) Heb. 4:1, - the importance of accepting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, the importance of understanding the passage of time and the transience of life on earth
> Let us therefore fear, lest, a promise being left us of entering into his rest, any of you should seem to come short of it. (Heb. 4:1, KJV)

### (2) Heb. 4:2, - the importance of understanding God's love and mercy
> For unto us was the gospel preached, as well as unto them: but the word preached did not profit them, not being mixed with faith in them that heard it. (Heb. 4:2, KJV)

#### (a) The proclamation of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ in all epochs of human history, the importance of accepting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ

#### (b) Hearing but not accepting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, meaningless life on earth, eternal destruction (the right to choose a person)

### (3) Heb. 4:3, - the importance of entering into the rest of God (salvation), the importance of understanding the only way to enter
> For we which have believed do enter into rest, as he said, As I have sworn in my wrath, if they shall enter into my rest: although the works were finished from the foundation of the world. (Heb. 4:3, KJV)

#### (a) Acceptance of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, the faith of God, Destiny

#### (b) His works were completed at the beginning of the world – God's plan of salvation, Christ slain from the creation of the world. Rev. 13:8,1 Pet. 1:18-20
> And all that dwell upon the earth shall worship him, whose names are not written in the book of life of the Lamb slain from the foundation of the world. (Rev. 13:8, KJV)
> Forasmuch as ye know that ye were not redeemed with corruptible things, as silver and gold, from your vain conversation received by tradition from your fathers; But with the precious blood of Christ, as of a lamb without blemish and without spot: Who verily was foreordained before the foundation of the world, but was manifest in these last times for you, (1 Pet. 1:18-20, KJV)

### (4) Heb. 4:4-6, - non-acceptance, rejection of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ (Jews), the impossibility of entering into the rest of God (salvation)
> For he spake in a certain place of the seventh day on this wise, And God did rest the seventh day from all his works. And in this place again, If they shall enter into my rest. Seeing therefore it remaineth that some must enter therein, and they to whom it was first preached entered not in because of unbelief: (Heb. 4:4-6, KJV)

### (5) Heb. 4:7, - the possibility of accepting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ (the Jews), the preaching of the apostles, the possibility of salvation
> Again, he limiteth a certain day, saying in David, To day, after so long a time; as it is said, To day if ye will hear his voice, harden not your hearts. (Heb. 4:7, KJV)

- ("Now that you have heard His voice, do not harden your hearts.")

### (6) Heb. 4:8, - The Conquest of Canaan (Joshua) It is not the entry into the rest of God (salvation), the importance of accepting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, the importance of fulfilling the destiny
> For if Jesus had given them rest, then would he not afterward have spoken of another day. (Heb. 4:8, KJV)

### (7) Heb. 4:9, - Sabbath day, the fulfillment of the Law for salvation, the rejection of Christ and His Sacrifice (being outside of rest), the problem of the Jews
> There remaineth therefore a rest to the people of God. (Heb. 4:9, KJV)

### (8) Heb. 4:10, - acceptance of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ (entering into rest), understanding the fulfillment of the Law by Christ instead of man, solving all problems, perfect peace and quiet
> For he that is entered into his rest, he also hath ceased from his own works, as God did from his. (Heb. 4:10, KJV)

### (9) Heb. 4:11, - the importance of accepting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, the importance of understanding the transience of life on earth
> Let us labour therefore to enter into that rest, lest any man fall after the same example of unbelief. (Heb. 4:11, KJV)

### (10) Heb. 4:12, - understanding the action of the Word of God (penetrates to the separation of soul and spirit, judges the thoughts of the heart),
> For the word of God is quick, and powerful, and sharper than any twoedged sword, piercing even to the dividing asunder of soul and spirit, and of the joints and marrow, and is a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart. (Heb. 4:12, KJV)

#### (a) The criterion of our faith, thoughts, desires and actions

#### (b) The importance of doing God's will, living a good life, serving God and people, fulfilling a destiny

### (11) Heb. 4:13, - the importance of understanding God for man
> Neither is there any creature that is not manifest in his sight: but all things are naked and opened unto the eyes of him with whom we have to do. (Heb. 4:13, KJV)

#### (a) The Omnipotence, Omniscience, and Omniscience of the Holy God

#### (b) The importance of understanding accountability to God for every human being

### (12) Heb. 4:14, - understanding of the Only High Priest of humanity, Jesus Christ, and the importance of professing faith. (The Divinity of Christ and the Sufficiency of His Sacrifice)
> Seeing then that we have a great high priest, that is passed into the heavens, Jesus the Son of God, let us hold fast our profession. (Heb. 4:14, KJV)

### (13) Heb. 4:15-16, - understanding the earthly life of Jesus Christ
> For we have not an high priest which cannot be touched with the feeling of our infirmities; but was in all points tempted like as we are, yet without sin. Let us therefore come boldly unto the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy, and find grace to help in time of need. (Heb. 4:15-16, KJV)

#### (a) Passing through all the temptations of a man who has never sinned, a true Man and a Holy God

#### (b) A true High Priest who understands a person's problem and is able to have compassion, give mercy, grace, and help.

#### (c) The importance of understanding the root cause of human life, the importance of faith, hope and trust in Jesus Christ
