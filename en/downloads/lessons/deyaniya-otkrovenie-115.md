## 2) Heb. 11:23-29, - the importance of understanding the faith of Moses
> By faith Moses, when he was born, was hid three months of his parents, because they saw he was a proper child; and they were not afraid of the king’s commandment. By faith Moses, when he was come to years, refused to be called the son of Pharaoh’s daughter; Choosing rather to suffer affliction with the people of God, than to enjoy the pleasures of sin for a season; Esteeming the reproach of Christ greater riches than the treasures in Egypt: for he had respect unto the recompence of the reward. By faith he forsook Egypt, not fearing the wrath of the king: for he endured, as seeing him who is invisible. Through faith he kept the passover, and the sprinkling of blood, lest he that destroyed the firstborn should touch them. By faith they passed through the Red sea as by dry land: which the Egyptians assaying to do were drowned. (Heb. 11:23-29, KJV)

#### (a) The faith of Moses' parents is the coming of Christ from the people of God

#### (b) The faith and choice of Moses – belonging to the people of God, renouncing the visible, understanding the coming of Christ from the people of God

#### (c) The faith of Moses, acceptance of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ – the fulfillment of Easter, understanding of the Sacrifice of Christ

#### (d) The faith of Moses – leaving Egypt, crossing the Red Sea, understanding the importance of Canaan, preparing for the coming of Christ

#### (e) The meaning of Moses' faith is Christ
