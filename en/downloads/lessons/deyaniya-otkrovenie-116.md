### (2) Heb. 12:4-11, - The importance of understanding God's punishment
> Ye have not yet resisted unto blood, striving against sin. And ye have forgotten the exhortation which speaketh unto you as unto children, My son, despise not thou the chastening of the Lord, nor faint when thou art rebuked of him: For whom the Lord loveth he chasteneth, and scourgeth every son whom he receiveth. #### (c) The importance of accepting God's punishment, understanding one's life, thoughts, emotions, and actions, and humbling oneself before God

#### (d) The importance of understanding the consequences of God's punishment – the peaceful fruit of righteousness, the awareness of mistakes and wrong things in the life of God's people, and the opportunity to serve God

#### (e) Resisting God's punishment – resisting God, not understanding the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, not understanding the will of God, separation from the family of God (not the children of God)
