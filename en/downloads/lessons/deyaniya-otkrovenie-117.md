## 2) Heb. 13:8-14, - the importance of understanding Christ and the action of N.Z.
> Jesus Christ the same yesterday, and to day, and for ever. Be not carried about with divers and strange doctrines. For it is a good thing that the heart be established with grace; not with meats, which have not profited them that have been occupied therein. We have an altar, whereof they have no right to eat which serve the tabernacle. For the bodies of those beasts, whose blood is brought into the sanctuary by the high priest for sin, are burned without the camp. Wherefore Jesus also, that he might sanctify the people with his own blood, suffered without the gate. Let us go forth therefore unto him without the camp, bearing his reproach. For here have we no continuing city, but we seek one to come. (Heb. 13:8-14, KJV)

### (1) Heb. 13:8, - the understanding of the constancy and immutability of Christ, God in the flesh, the fullness of the Godhead bodily, the image of the invisible God
> Jesus Christ the same yesterday, and to day, and for ever. (Heb. 13:8, KJV)

## 3) Heb. 13:15-25, - the importance of fulfilling the purpose
> By him therefore let us offer the sacrifice of praise to God continually, that is, the fruit of our lips giving thanks to his name. But to do good and to communicate forget not: for with such sacrifices God is well pleased. Obey them that have the rule over you, and submit yourselves: for they watch for your souls, as they that must give account, that they may do it with joy, and not with grief: for that is unprofitable for you. Pray for us: for we trust we have a good conscience, in all things willing to live honestly. But I beseech you the rather to do this, that I may be restored to you the sooner. Now the God of peace, that brought again from the dead our Lord Jesus, that great shepherd of the sheep, through the blood of the everlasting covenant, Make you perfect in every good work to do his will, working in you that which is wellpleasing in his sight, through Jesus Christ; to whom be glory for ever and ever. Amen. And I beseech you, brethren, suffer the word of exhortation: for I have written a letter unto you in few words. Know ye that our brother Timothy is set at liberty; with whom, if he come shortly, I will see you. Salute all them that have the rule over you, and all the saints. They of Italy salute you. Grace be with you all. Amen. (Heb. 13:15-25, KJV)

## Conclusion:

### (1) Heb. 1-2, - Christ is above the Angels (prayers to the Angels)

### (2) Heb. 3, - Christ is higher than Moses (prayers to the "saints")

### (3) Heb. 4, - Christ is above Isa. Joshua (trust in spiritual leaders)

### (4) Heb. 4, - Christ is above the Law (understanding of the Sabbath, circumcision.)

### (5) Heb. 5-7, - Christ is above the Levitical priesthood, the eternal High Priest according to the order of Melchizedek, the only Surety and Intercessor (trust in the human priesthood)

### (6) Heb. 8, - Christ is the best Covenant (cancellation of V.Z., action of N.Z.)

### (7) Heb. 9-10, - Christ is the best Tabernacle and the best Sacrifice

### (8) Heb. 11, - Christ is the meaning of God's faith for man

### (9) Heb. 12, - Christ is the Ruler and Finisher of the faith of God's people (the importance of understanding God's upbringing)

### (10) Heb. 12-13, - Christ, this is the meaning of knowledge and transmission of the Word of God, the Testament, Christ John 5:39
> Search the scriptures; for in them ye think ye have eternal life: and they are they which testify of me. (John 5:39, KJV)
