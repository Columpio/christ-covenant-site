## 2) Rev. 2, - God is the sovereignty and control of the life of God's people. (An appeal to specific churches of that time, and based on these examples, an appeal to the Universal Church at all times)

### (1) Epistle to the Church of Ephesus. Rev. 2:1-7
> Unto the angel of the church of Ephesus write; These things saith he that holdeth the seven stars in his right hand, who walketh in the midst of the seven golden candlesticks; I know thy works, and thy labour, and thy patience, and how thou canst not bear them which are evil: and thou hast tried them which say they are apostles, and are not, and hast found them liars: And hast borne, and hast patience, and for my name’s sake hast laboured, and hast not fainted. Nevertheless I have somewhat against thee, because thou hast left thy first love. Remember therefore from whence thou art fallen, and repent, and do the first works; or else I will come unto thee quickly, and will remove thy candlestick out of his place, except thou repent. But this thou hast, that thou hatest the deeds of the Nicolaitans, which I also hate. He that hath an ear, let him hear what the Spirit saith unto the churches; To him that overcometh will I give to eat of the tree of life, which is in the midst of the paradise of God. (Rev. 2:1-7, KJV)

#### (a) Praise – preservation, transmission of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, purity of teaching (Rejection of false apostles, Nicolaitans, patience, work for God)

#### (b) Chastisement – abandoning one's first love. (First love, agape – constant knowledge and transmission of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, fulfillment of the destiny of the people of God)

#### (c) The possibility of repentance and the importance of returning to the destiny of God's people – God's love and mercy

#### (d) God's reward is the tree of life, eternal life with Christ. (The importance of hearing and fulfilling the Word of God for the people of God)

## Result:

### (1) Jesus Christ's constant attention to the Church - the importance of knowing and transmitting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, the disciples, World Evangelization

### (2) God's praise of the Church (emphasis) – purity of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, fulfillment of the destiny of the people of God

### (3) God's chastisement of the Church (emphasis) – there is no purity of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ (other teachings), there is no fulfillment of destiny

### (4) God's reward for the Church (emphasis on the invisible, the transience of the visible) is Emmanuel, eternity with God

### (5) Christ's constant attention to unsaved people – "standing and knocking", the importance of human salvation. Rev. 3:20
