# 10. Rev. 21-22, - Understanding Eternity

## 1) The order of events of the Book of Revelation

### (1) The Ministry of Christ, the message to the seven Churches, the importance of knowing and transmitting the Word of God, understanding the future. Rev. 1-3

### (2) The kingdom of heaven, God's plan, and God's sovereignty. Rev. 4-5

### (3) 1st half of the Last Week, the Rapture of C. Rev. 6-12

### (4) The coming and enthronement of the Antichrist. Rev. 13

### (5) The 2nd half of the Last Week, the judgments of God. Rev. 14-16

### (6) The 2nd half of the Last Week, God's judgment on Religion. Rev. 17-18

### (7) The Second Coming of Christ, the 1000th kingdom. Rev. 19-20

### (8) Judgment is at the Great Throne. Rev. 20
