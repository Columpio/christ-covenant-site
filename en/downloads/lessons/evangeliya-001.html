2) The importance of the Four Gospels

(1) The Four Gospels – God's sovereignty, the importance of Christ's coming and His ministry, and evidence of the truth of events

(2) The Gospels of Matthew and John – a detailed and extensive description of events (related to the twelve apostles and therefore considered the main Gospels)

(3) The Gospels of Mark and Luke complement the main descriptions of events (they did not relate to the twelve apostles and are therefore considered additional Gospels)

(4) The importance of understanding the order of events in the life of Christ

(1) Birth and preparation for ministry (Bethlehem, Nazareth, 30 l.) John 1,Matt. 1-2 (Luke 1-2)
