## 2) John 3:22-36, - the importance of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, John the Baptist

Then there arose a question between some of John’s disciples and the Jews about purifying. And they came unto John, and said unto him, Rabbi, he that was with thee beyond Jordan, to whom thou barest witness, behold, the same baptizeth, and all men come to him.
