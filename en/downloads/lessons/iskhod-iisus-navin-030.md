### (2) The importance of understanding the genealogy of Jesus Christ

#### (a) Matt. 1:1-17, - the genealogy of Jesus Christ, the line of Joseph
> The book of the generation of Jesus Christ, the son of David, the son of Abraham. So all the generations from Abraham to David are fourteen generations; and from David until the carrying away into Babylon are fourteen generations; and from the carrying away into Babylon unto Christ are fourteen generations.
