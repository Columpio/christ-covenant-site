## 8) Heresies concerning Christ

### (1) The heresy of Arians (Arianism), Macedonians (Doukhobors) and Eumenomians, Anomaeans (1st and 2nd Ecumenical Councils) – denial of the Divinity of Christ and the Holy Spirit, denial of the Trinity of the Holy, True God. (God the Father is the true God, God the Son is a created person, God the Holy Spirit is a created person or divine energy)

#### (a) Christ is divine and human. nature is inseparably united in one Person, without transformation or mixing (The visible image of the Holy and Triune God, the fullness of the Deity physically – the ability to see and understand God)

#### (b) Fulfillment of the Word of God – Jesus Christ as God and Creator (the right to be responsible for his creation), and as a perfect, sinless Man in the likeness of sinful flesh (the right to accept suffering and death for all the sins of mankind) Col. 1:15-17,Eph. 3:9, - 1 Tim. 3:16,Rom. 8:3, - Heb. 2:9; 10:14
> Who is the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of every creature: For by him were all things created, that are in heaven, and that are in earth, visible and invisible, whether they be thrones, or dominions, or principalities, or powers: all things were created by him, and for him: And he is before all things, and by him all things consist. (Col. 1:15-17, KJV)
> And to make all men see what is the fellowship of the mystery, which from the beginning of the world hath been hid in God, who created all things by Jesus Christ: (Eph. 3:9, KJV)
> And without controversy great is the mystery of godliness: God was manifest in the flesh, justified in the Spirit, seen of angels, preached unto the Gentiles, believed on in the world, received up into glory. (1 Tim. 3:16, KJV)
> For what the law could not do, in that it was weak through the flesh, God sending his own Son in the likeness of sinful flesh, and for sin, condemned sin in the flesh: (Rom. 8:3, KJV)
> But we see Jesus, who was made a little lower than the angels for the suffering of death, crowned with glory and honour; that he by the grace of God should taste death for every man. (Heb. 2:9, KJV)
> For by one offering he hath perfected for ever them that are sanctified. (Heb. 10:14, KJV)

#### (c) Denial of the Divine nature of Christ – there is no right to be responsible for one's creation, there is no salvation for mankind

#### (d) Faith in another, non–biblical Christ (100% Human, and 100% God) is a sin leading to death, the impossibility of salvation

#### (e) Denial of the Trinity of the Holy, True God – faith not in the God of the Bible, i.e. faith in another god (impossibility of salvation)

### (2) Monophylitism (6th Ecumenical Council) - denial of the human nature of Christ, absence of human will in Jesus Christ, only Divine will. Matt. 26:39, 42

#### (a) Fulfillment of the Word of God – Jesus Christ as God and Creator (the right to be responsible for his creation), and as a perfect, sinless Man in the likeness of sinful flesh (the right to accept suffering and death for all the sins of mankind)

#### (b) Denial of the human nature of Christ (absence of human will) – there is no execution of the sentence of the Word of God, there is no payment for sin, there is no salvation of mankind

#### (c) Faith in another, non–biblical Christ (100% Human, and 100% God) is a sin leading to death, the impossibility of salvation

### (3) Apollinarianism (2nd Ecumenical Council) – denial of the human nature of Jesus Christ. (Body is human nature, mind and spirit are Divine nature)

#### (a) Fulfillment of the Word of God – Jesus Christ as God and Creator (the right to be responsible for his creation), and as a perfect, sinless Man in the likeness of sinful flesh (the right to accept suffering and death for all the sins of mankind)

#### (b) The denial of the human nature of Christ (the absence of a human soul and spirit) – there is no fulfillment of the sentence of the Word of God, there is no payment for sin, there is no salvation of mankind

#### (c) Faith in another, non–biblical Christ (100% Human, and 100% God) is a sin leading to death, the impossibility of salvation

### (4) Nestorianism (archep. Nestorius, 4th Ecumenical Council) – incorrect teaching about two persons in Jesus Christ

#### (a) Fulfillment of the Word of God – Jesus Christ as God and Creator (the right to be responsible for his creation), and as a perfect, sinless Man in the likeness of sinful flesh (the right to accept suffering and death for all the sins of mankind)

#### (b) Two persons in Christ – man together with God, a denial of the Divinity of Christ. (The divine and human natures were inseparably united in one Person of Christ, without transformation or mixing)

#### (c) Denial of the Divine nature of Christ – there is no right to be responsible for one's creation, there is no salvation for mankind

#### (d) Faith in another, non–biblical Christ (100% Human, and 100% God) is a sin leading to death, the impossibility of salvation

### (5) Monophysitism (Eutychianism, 4th Ecumenical Council) – the denial of the human nature of Jesus Christ, absorbed by the divine, the change of both natures and the formation of a third kind of nature, i.e. Jesus Christ is not 100% God and not 100% human, but it is not clear who. (Affirmation of the presence of two natures in Jesus Christ – the divine and human natures were inseparably united in one person or one person of Jesus Christ, without transformation or mixing)

#### (a) Fulfillment of the Word of God – Jesus Christ as God and Creator (the right to be responsible for his creation), and as a perfect, sinless Man in the likeness of sinful flesh (the right to accept suffering and death for all the sins of mankind)

#### (a) Fulfillment of the Word of God – Jesus Christ as God and Creator (the right to be responsible for his creation), and as a perfect, sinless Man in the likeness of sinful flesh (the right to accept suffering and death for all the sins of mankind)
