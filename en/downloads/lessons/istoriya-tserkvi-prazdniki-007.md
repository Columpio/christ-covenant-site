## 1) I believe in One God, the Father Almighty, Creator of heaven and earth, of all things visible and invisible

### (1) Faith in One God who is numerically one and in whom there are three eternal differences – the Personality of God the Father, the Personality of God the Word (Son), the Personality of God the Holy Spirit, each of whom is God and equal in Power and Glory, manifesting Himself simultaneously and jointly in three Hypostases, without the possibility of separation in Himself – the Holy and Indivisible Trinity, the Beginningless and Infinite Saint, the Triune God. (Three eternal differences in the human being – spirit, soul and body)

### (2) Faith in God the Father as the First Hypostasis of the Triune God

### (3) Faith in One God as the Creator of the spiritual and material world with his own laws

### (4) Faith in God's sovereignty and control – God's plan, God's Will, and God's plan for creation. Jer. 29:11
> For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, saith the LORD, thoughts of peace, and not of evil, to give you an expected end. (Jer. 29:11, KJV)
