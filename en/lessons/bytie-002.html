  • V.Z. – faith in the coming Christ (God in the flesh) and the All-sufficiency of His Sacrifice (Covenant, Christ, animal sacrifice)
  • N.Z. – faith in the coming Jesus as Christ (God in the flesh) and the All-sufficiency of His Sacrifice (Covenant, Christ, Communion)
