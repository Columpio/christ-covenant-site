(c) A person's personal responsibility in following the Word of God's Disobedience – other teachings, idols (Adam, Solomon)

Conclusion:

(1) Grace is God's action, the undeserved grace of Communion with God

(2) The action of God's grace is the proclamation of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ

(3) Acceptance of the grace of God – knowledge, transmission of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ

(4) A person without the grace of God is unable to live, meet, reconcile, and be with God (100% dependence on God)

(5) A person can resist the grace of God Acts 7:51; 13:46,Matt. 23:37
