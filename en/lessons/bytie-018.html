18. Repentance, Repentance and Confession of sins

Contents
  1. 1) Salvation is Emmanuel, God together with man. (Rebirth, restoration to the original form, lawful liberation from the power of sin, death and hell, Satan) Rom. 8:2
  2. 2) Repentance – assessment of actions, understanding of sin, emotions, lack of acceptance of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, there is no salvation and rebirth (Judas) Matt. 27:3-5
