8) Practical issues for personal evangelization and testing of every person

(1) Do you believe in God? – the importance of understanding a person’s general position in relation to God

(2) Which God do you believe in? – the importance of understanding a person's faith

(3) If you die now (heart attack), will you be with God? – verification of the understanding of salvation, the hope of man (against the heresy of the "mixed gospel")

(4) Can there be curses and demons in those born again? – verification of the understanding of salvation, the hope of man (against the heresy of "partial salvation")

(5) What is the purpose of the people of God (the Church of Christ)? – verification of the understanding of the destiny of the people of God (against a misunderstanding of the destiny of the Church of Christ)

Result:

(1) Depending on the answers, we understand a person's spiritual state (the importance of a spiritual diagnosis). Matt. 12:37

9) The importance of understanding the signs of human salvation

(1) The confession of man is the acceptance of the Divinity of Christ and the All–sufficiency of His Sacrifice

(2) Knowledge of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ (very strong interest in the Word of God), human communion with the people of God (local Church)

(3) Transmission of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, Preaching

(4) Participation in God's Sacraments (Water Baptism, Communion)

(5) The changed life of man – service to God and people (process)

Result:

(1) The absence of a person's correct profession means that there is no possibility of salvation even if all other points are present (Jehovah's Witnesses, Orthodoxy, Catholicism, etc.)

(2) A person's confession without the other points is just knowledge without accepting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, there is no possibility of salvation

Conclusion:

(1) The importance of accepting the Word of God, the Covenant, and the Christ–confession of man

(2) Understanding the work of Satan – resisting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ

(3) The importance of fulfilling the destiny of God's people – knowing, transmitting, and explaining the Word of God, the Covenant, and Christ
