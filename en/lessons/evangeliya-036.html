31. Matthew 21 chapters. (The last week of the ministry of Jesus Christ)

Contents
  1. 1) Matt. 21:1-11 (Zech. 9:9), - fulfillment of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ (Palm Sunday – the arrival of Jesus Christ in Jerusalem) Gen. 1:27; 2:7, 17 (Gen. 3:1-7, - Rom. 5:12; 6:23,Eph. 2:2), - Gen. 3:15, 21
  2. 2) Matt. 21:10-11, - the importance of accepting Jesus Christ as a true Prophet for every person, proclaiming the Word of God
  3. 3) Matt. 21:12-13 (John 2:12-17), - the importance of accepting Jesus Christ as the High Priest of the people of God, solving the problem of sin. (Christ's constant attention to the temple of God – man, the importance of Emmanuel, man's unwillingness to be the temple of God)
  4. 4) The importance of accepting Jesus Christ as the true and only King, Priest and Prophet for humanity
  5. 5) Matt. 21:18-22, - fulfillment of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ
  6. 6) Matt. 21:23-46, - rejection of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, the parable of the sons
  7. 7) Matt. 21:33-46, - non-acceptance of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, destiny
  8. Conclusion:
