2) The importance of understanding Christ in Leviticus

(1) Christ as God's Servant

(2) Christ as the Old Testament – the Law, the Tabernacle, the Feasts

(3) Christ as a Sacrifice that solves all human problems

(4) Christ as the One who ordains Priests and watches over the purity of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ

(5) Christ as Pure for man

(6) Christ as the Healer of Leprosy

(7) Christ as the Only Purification of the natural uncleanness of every human being

(8) Christ, as the true High Priest, is the forgiveness and purification of every human being, the removal of all sins, and the only Mediator and Intercessor of every human being.

(9) Christ as the Only and Common Divine Service for the children of God

(10) Christ as the Only Sacrifice and Blood for the salvation of every human being

(11) Christ as the Only Priest of every human being – the importance of solving a spiritual problem

(12) Christ as God's Plan – Past, Present and Future

(13) Christ as Daily Worship, the True Light and True Bread for every human being

(14) Christ as the Sabbath is perfect Rest.

(15) Christ as Jubilee Year – return to its Original form

(16) Christ as a Choice. Lev. 26

(17) Christ as the Provider of every man
