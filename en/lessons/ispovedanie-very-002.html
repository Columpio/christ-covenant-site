1) The prayer "Our Father" and "Symbol of Faith" at the beginning of the Divine Service

(1) I believe in One God, the Father Almighty, Creator of heaven and earth, of all things visible and invisible.

(2) And in the One Lord Jesus Christ, the Image of the Invisible God, the Fullness of the Godhead bodily, the Word of God in the flesh, the Son of God, the Eternal, the Only Begotten, begotten of the Father before all ages, God of God, Light of Light, True God of True God, begotten, uncreated, consubstantial with the Father, through whom all things were created

(3) For the sake of us humans, and for the sake of our salvation, who descended from heaven and was incarnate of the Holy Spirit and the Virgin Mary and became a Man in the likeness of sinful flesh.

(4) Crucified for all the sins of mankind – past, present and future under Pontius Pilate, suffered and buried (Lamb of God, Easter of the Lord),

(5) Who rose on the third day according to the Scriptures,

(6) Ascended into heaven and sits at the right hand of the Father – the only King, High Priest, Prophet, Teacher and Mentor, Redeemer and Savior, Mediator and Intercessor, who solved all the problems of the human race, who gives not by works, but only by grace and faith – forgiveness of all sins, justification, salvation and rebirth, adoption and church formation to every person, the Only Way, Truth, Resurrection and Life

(7) He will come again with glory to judge the living and the dead, and His kingdom will have no end.

(8) And in the Holy Spirit, the Lord, the Giver of Life, who proceeds from the Father and the Son, to whom worship and glory belong together with the Father and the Son, who spoke through the prophets in the Holy Scriptures of the Old and New Testaments.

(9) And in the One, Holy, Universal, and Apostolic Church, whose head is the Lord God Jesus Christ, I believe in God's order of worship in the Apostolic Church of Jesus Christ–the teaching of the Apostles, communion, the breaking of bread, prayer.

(10) I confess the one Breaking of Bread and the one Baptism in the name of Jesus Christ, which means equally in the name of the Father and the Son., and the Holy Spirit as a visible testimony of abiding in the Covenant of God and the forgiveness of all sins – past, present and future. I believe in the importance of knowing and transmitting the Word of God, the Covenant, and Christ to the ends of the earth.

(11) I await the Second Coming of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, the resurrection of the dead,

(12) And the life of the next century. And may all the people of God say, Amen.

4) The prayer of the Church at the end of the Divine Service

(1) Father, bless your Church, and may Your will be done in our lives, and may all the people of God say: Amen.

(2) Father, bless the children of your people, and may your will be done in the lives of our children, and may all the people of God say: Amen.

(3) Father, heal your church, and may your will be done in our healing, and may all the people of God say: Amen.

(4) Father, may financial order come to your Church. We thank you that we have everything we need for life and piety in Jesus Christ, and may all the people of God say: Amen.

(5) Father, have mercy and admonish the apostates, convert them to yourself, and may your will be done in their lives, and may all the people of God say: Amen.

(6) Father, bless the unsaved people, give them the opportunity to hear Your Word and make their choice, and may Your will be done in their lives – and may all the people of God say: Amen

(7) Father, bless our enemies and may your will be done in their lives, and may all the people of God say: Amen.

(8) Father, bless our people, our country and our government and let Your will be done for us and for our land - and let all the people of God say: Amen

(9) Lord, for all things we thank and praise you, the Great, Eternal, Holy, and True God: the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit, through Jesus Christ, and may all the people of God say: Amen

5) The blessing of God's people. Num. 6:22-27

And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying, Speak unto Aaron and unto his sons, saying, On this wise ye shall bless the children of Israel, saying unto them, The LORD bless thee, and keep thee: The LORD make his face shine upon thee, and be gracious unto thee: The LORD lift up his countenance upon thee, and give thee peace. And they shall put my name upon the children of Israel; and I will bless them.

Num. 6:22-27 · KJV

Then the LORD said to Moses, “Tell Aaron and his sons: This is how you are to bless the Israelites. Say to them: ‘May the LORD bless you and keep you; may the LORD cause His face to shine upon you and be gracious to you; may the LORD lift up His countenance toward you and give you peace.’ So they shall put My name on the Israelites, and I will bless them.”

Num. 6:22-27 · BSB

Yahweh spoke to Moses, saying, “Speak to Aaron and to his sons, saying, ‘This is how you shall bless the children of Israel.’ You shall tell them, ‘Yahweh bless you, and keep you. Yahweh make his face to shine on you, and be gracious to you. Yahweh lift up his face toward you, and give you peace.’ “So they shall put my name on the children of Israel; and I will bless them.”

Num. 6:22-27 · WEB

(1) May the Lord bless you and keep you safe!

(2) May the Lord look upon you with a bright face and have mercy on you!

(3) May the Lord set his face on you and give you peace!
