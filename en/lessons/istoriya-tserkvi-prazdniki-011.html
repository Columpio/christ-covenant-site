4) The main world creeds – the right to choose a person, the understanding of God's sovereignty and control

(1) Hinduism – After the Flood, India

(a) Belief in the One God, Brahman, who manifests himself in various forms and forms – Vishnu, Krishna, Shiva, Rama (the appearance of God to other nations in various forms and personalities – stone, tree, Christ, Prophet Muhammad, etc.)

(b) Understanding of good and evil, faith in human responsibility, understanding of the temporality of the visible, faith in eternity

(c) The method of salvation (union with God, liberation from evil) is man's deeds and deeds, righteousness and salvation from works. (Belief in reincarnation – multiple rebirths of a person)

(d) The attitude towards Jesus Christ is one of the many images in which the "god Brahman" appeared to people

  • The result is a rejection of the Divinity of Christ and the All–sufficiency of His Sacrifice (inconsistency with the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, another word)

(2) Buddhism – 6th century B.C., India

(a) Denial of the existence of God (the world is neither created nor controlled by anyone, does the world have a beginning? – there is no response)

(b) Through deeds, a person frees himself from matter, evil and suffering (the main goal of Buddhism) and enters an eternal state of peace and bliss – nirvana. (Belief in reincarnation – multiple rebirths of a person)

(c) Attitude towards Jesus Christ – an enlightened person

  • The result is a rejection of the Divinity of Christ and the All–sufficiency of His Sacrifice (inconsistency with the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, another word)

(3) Judaism – 2nd century B.C., the prophet Abraham and Moses

(a) Faith in the One God

(b) Understanding of good and evil, faith in human responsibility, understanding of the temporality of the visible, faith in eternity

(c) The method of salvation (union with God, liberation from evil) is man's deeds and deeds, righteousness and salvation from works

(d) Attitude towards Jesus Christ – a man, a false messiah

  • The result is a rejection of the Divinity of Christ and the All–sufficiency of His Sacrifice (inconsistency with the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, another word)

(4) Christian religion (Catholicism, Orthodoxy, currents of distorted Protestantism) – 1st century A.D., affirmation of the heresies of the "mixed gospel" and "partial salvation"

(a) Faith in the One God

(b) Understanding of good and evil, faith in human responsibility, understanding of the temporality of the visible, faith in eternity

(c) The method of salvation (union with God, liberation from evil) is man's deeds and deeds, righteousness and salvation from works

(d) The attitude towards Jesus Christ, the God–Man, the affirmation of the insufficiency of Christ's Sacrifice for human salvation

  • The result is acceptance of the Divinity of Christ, but not acceptance of the All–sufficiency of His Sacrifice (inconsistency is the Word of God, another word)

(5) Islam – 7th century A.D., Prophet Muhammad, Arabia

(a) Faith in the One God

(b) Understanding of good and evil, faith in human responsibility, understanding of the temporality of the visible, faith in eternity

(c) The method of salvation (union with God, liberation from evil) is man's deeds and deeds, righteousness and salvation from works

(d) Attitude towards Jesus Christ – a man (a prophet), another man died on the cross instead of Jesus Christ (God protecting the prophet)

  • The result is a rejection of the Divinity of Christ and the All–sufficiency of His Sacrifice (inconsistency with the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, another word)

(6) Atheism is an antediluvian civilization, giants

(a) Denial of God and the spiritual world

(b) Understanding of good and evil, understanding of human responsibility

(c) Through fundamental science (business) to achieve mastery over the universe, disease, aging, life and death. (The importance of a person's spiritual, physical, and mental development)

(d) Attitude towards Jesus Christ – a good man who fought for goodness and justice

  • The result is a rejection of the Divinity of Christ and the All–sufficiency of His Sacrifice (inconsistency with the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, another word)

Conclusion:

(1) Understanding the commonality (one meaning) of all major religions

(a) Understanding of good and evil, human responsibility, faith in eternity

(b) Human deeds and deeds are the only way to solve all human problems ("you will be like God yourself")

(2) Understanding the opposition to the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ

(a) The rejection of Christ as God in the flesh

(b) Rejection of Christ's Sacrifice as God's only method of saving humanity

(3) Understanding the Reason for God's Acceptance of Religions and Teachings

(a) Understanding a person's right to choose is a gift from God

(b) Understanding God's sovereignty and control – the possibility of actually exercising a person's right to choose
