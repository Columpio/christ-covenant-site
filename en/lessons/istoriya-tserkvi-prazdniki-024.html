2) Num. 1-10, - the importance of understanding and fulfilling the purpose

(1) Num. 1-2, - the importance of spiritual warfare for the people of God

(2) Num. 3-4, - the importance of Worship and the participation of the people of God

(3) Num. 5-6, - the importance of sanctification and dedication of the people to God

(4) Num. 7, - the importance of financial service to the people of God

(5) Num. 8, - the importance of the priesthood and Levitical ministry. (Pastor, Deacon – knowledge and transmission of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, World Evangelization)

(6) Num. 9, - The importance of understanding Easter and God's guidance

(7) Num. 10, - the importance of the trumpet call. (Purpose is a constant call to fulfill the Word of God)

3) Num. 11-16, - opposition to the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, World Evangelization

(1) Num. 11, - insufficiency of manna (The Word of God, the Covenant, Christ)

(2) Num. 12, - the rebellion of Aaron and Miriam (God of Sovereignty)

(3) Num. 12-14, - the Spies and Joshua, Caleb

(4) Num. 15, - rejection of the Word of God, the importance of the seventh day

(5) Num. 16, - the rebellion of Korah, Dathan and Aviron

4) Num. 17-19, - the importance of understanding the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ

(1) Num. 17, - understanding of the Gods (Aaron's rod)

(2) Num. 18, - understanding the economics of Central Asia

(3) Num. 19, - understanding the uniqueness of the victim for the victim. human

5) Num. 20-25, 31, - inaccurate fulfillment of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ

(1) Num. 20, - the importance of faithfully fulfilling God's Word and understanding God's sovereignty (death of Aaron, Miriam)

(2) Num. 21, - opposition to the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ (the brazen serpent)

(3) Num. 22-25, 31, - Idols, loss of salvation (Balaam)

6) Num. 26-30, - the importance of knowing and transmitting the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ

(1) Num. 26, - Affirmation of destiny (spiritual warfare)

(2) Num. 27, - the importance of the disciple (Joshua)

(3) Num. 28-29, - the importance of Sacrifice and proper Worship

(4) Num. 29-30, - the importance of God of order in the families of the people of God

(5) Num. 31, - the importance of understanding God's judgment (acceptance of the Covenant, Christ)

(6) Num. 32-35, - the importance of Canaan to the people of God (intended)

(7) Num. 36 (27), - the importance of preserving God's people, understanding and fulfilling their destiny (faith of the Salpaadov daughters)
