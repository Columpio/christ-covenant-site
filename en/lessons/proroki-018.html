(1) 1st stage – Zerubbabel Temple (538 BC)

(a) Cyrus the Great II (539-536 BC) – the fulfillment of the Word of God, the Covenant, Christ, the return of the Jews to Canaan, the coming of Christ

(b) Cambyses the 2nd or Artaxerxes (536-522 BC) – the action of Satan, stopping the construction of the Temple, resisting the fulfillment of the Word of God

(c) Darius the 1st (522-486 BC) – completion of the Temple (516)

(d) Xerxes the 1st or Artaxerxes (485-464 BC), Esther's husband

(2) 2nd stage – Ezra, the Writings of V.Z. (458 BC) (King Artaxerxes 1,464-423 BC)

(3) Stage 3 – Nehemiah, the walls of Jerusalem (444 BC) (King Artaxerxes 1,464-423 BC)
